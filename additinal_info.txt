The moment TRU Connect touches money movement, the architecture changes completely—not because you're becoming a bank, but because attackers now have a financial incentive.

The important distinction is this:

TRU Connect today → records business activity (debts, orders, suppliers, repayments). It does not own money.
Payment Gateway → moves the money (Paystack, Ozow, Peach Payments, Stitch, etc.).
Supplier's Bank → ultimately receives the money.
TRU Connect → orchestrates and verifies the process.

That means the Shared Money Engine is not a banking engine. It is the platform's financial integrity engine.
