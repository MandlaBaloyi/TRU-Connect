It is not an implementation guide.

If any document, design decision, software implementation, business decision, or future proposal conflicts with this Constitution, this Constitution SHALL take precedence.

TruePoint is intended to evolve over decades.

Therefore, every decision SHALL prioritize long-term platform evolution over short-term convenience.

===========================================================================
PART I
WHY TRUEPOINT EXISTS
===========================================================================

"There are companies that begin with ideas.

There are companies that begin with products.

TruePoint began with a discovery."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-001
The Discovery Before The Company
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TruePoint was never created because someone wanted to build another technology company.

It was created because a deeper problem was discovered.

The original objective was to improve access to finance for informal businesses.

The first attempt to solve that problem was through machine learning.

The mathematics worked.

The predictive models performed well.

However, one critical question remained unanswered.

"Why would anyone trust the prediction?"

That question fundamentally changed the direction of the project.

Instead of improving the model, the focus shifted toward understanding the real problem.

That discovery became the beginning of TruePoint.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-002
Technology Is Not The Beginning
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TruePoint did not begin with software.

It did not begin with mobile applications.

It did not begin with payments.

It did not begin with lending.

Technology was never the objective.

Technology became necessary only after understanding the problem.

TruePoint exists because solving the underlying problem required building technology that previously did not exist.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-003
The Company's Mission
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The mission of TruePoint is to build trusted digital infrastructure that enables informal businesses to participate confidently in the formal financial ecosystem.

Everything built within the TruePoint ecosystem SHALL contribute toward this mission.

No product SHALL exist independently of this purpose.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Constitutional Principle

TruePoint SHALL always pursue root causes rather than symptoms.

Products are temporary.

Infrastructure compounds.

Trust compounds.

The platform SHALL always optimize for long-term trust creation.

PART VIII
PLATFORM ARCHITECTURE CONSTITUTION
===========================================================================

"Architecture determines how long software survives."

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-031
One Platform
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TruePoint SHALL operate as a single platform.

Every product SHALL share common infrastructure.

Products SHALL extend the platform.

They SHALL NOT become isolated systems.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-032
One User Identity
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every individual interacting with the ecosystem SHALL exist once.

The platform SHALL maintain a single Users table.

Authentication SHALL remain centralized.

Authorization SHALL be Role-Based.

Businesses.

Suppliers.

Supplier Staff.

Enterprise Users.

Lenders.

Administrators.

Future roles.

SHALL all authenticate using the same identity platform.

Capabilities SHALL be determined through roles and permissions rather than duplicate user implementations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-033
Domain Driven Design
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The platform SHALL be organized around business domains.

Each domain SHALL own:

Its models.

Its services.

Its business rules.

Its APIs.

Its database interactions.

Domains SHOULD communicate through clearly defined interfaces.

Business logic SHALL remain inside the owning domain.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-034
Modular Monolith First
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The initial architecture SHALL be a Modular Monolith.

Domains SHALL remain isolated internally.

The architecture SHALL allow future migration toward distributed services if operational scale requires it.

Premature microservices SHALL be avoided.

Architecture SHALL evolve only when justified by business growth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-035
API First
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Every platform capability SHALL be exposed through well-defined APIs.

Applications SHALL consume platform APIs.

Future products SHALL reuse existing APIs whenever practical.

APIs SHALL remain versioned.

Backward compatibility SHALL be preserved whenever possible.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-036
Event Driven Evolution
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Platform events SHALL become the preferred communication mechanism between domains.

Examples include:

Order Created

Order Completed

Payment Confirmed

Supplier Added

Inventory Updated

Business Verified

Loan Approved

Delivery Confirmed

This allows future services to evolve without tightly coupling platform components.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

TP-037
Real-Time Platform
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The platform SHALL support real-time communication where operational value exists.

Examples include:

Order status.

Driver updates.

Supplier notifications.

Business notifications.

Inventory updates.

Loan status.

WebSockets SHALL be preferred for real-time platform communication.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Architecture Constitutional Principles

One Platform.

One Identity.

One Engineering Standard.

Shared Platform Services.

Domain Ownership.

Event Driven Evolution.

API First.

Real-Time By Design.
