================================================================================
1. BUSINESS REGISTRATION  [IMPLEMENTED]
================================================================================

    1. User selects "Business Owner" during onboarding.
    2. Registers with minimal fields: first name, last name, email,
       password. Optional business name.
    3. Account created: role = business, verification_status =
       unverified, account_status = active.
    4. Welcome email sent (never blocks registration).
    5. OTP verification.
    6. Business Profile Setup.
    7. Prompted to ADD MY SUPPLIERS (Section 5).

Rules:
    - One email owns exactly one account and one role. A single email may
      not own both a business and a supplier account.
    - Registration collects only what is necessary. Business detail is
      collected later, in Profile Setup, to minimise abandonment.
    - Verification is not required to place orders or use the Loan Book.
      Requiring it before value would violate TP-019.

================================================================================
2. SUPPLIER REGISTRATION  [IMPLEMENTED]
================================================================================

    1. User selects "Supplier", or accepts an invitation from a business.
    2. Registers with minimal fields: owner name, email, password.
       Optional business name.
    3. Account created: role = supplier, tier = free.
    4. Welcome email sent.
    5. OTP verification.
    6. Supplier Profile Setup.
    7. If arriving by invitation, the inviting business's
       business_suppliers record links automatically (Section 6).

Rules:
    - Supplier Free is fully functional for receiving and fulfilling
      orders. Tier gates additional capability, never core order flow.

================================================================================
3. VERIFICATION  [IMPLEMENTED]
================================================================================

    1. Business or supplier submits verification documents.
    2. verification_request created with status = pending.
    3. Reviewed (Phase 1: manual).
    4. Approved -> status approved, is_verified set true, badge appears.
       Rejected -> status rejected, reviewer notes retained internally;
       the user sees only a generic "additional information required".
    5. Notification sent; verification_history records the transition.

================================================================================
4. AUTHENTICATION LIFECYCLE  [IMPLEMENTED]
================================================================================

    Login: case-insensitive email; identical error for unknown email and
    wrong password (enumeration protection); last_login_at updated;
    access + refresh tokens issued.

    Refresh: presented refresh token validated against the database,
    revoked, and a NEW pair issued (rotation). A reused refresh token is
    rejected.

    Logout: revokes the specified refresh token, or all active refresh
    tokens for the user if none specified.

    Password reset: enumeration-safe request; single-use token; on
    success, password updated AND all refresh tokens revoked, forcing
    re-login everywhere.

    OTP: expires (10 minutes), attempt-limited (5), single use.

================================================================================
5. ADD EXISTING SUPPLIER   [CORE WORKFLOW]
================================================================================

This is the defining workflow of TRUConnect.

    1. Business taps "Add Supplier".
    2. Enters supplier name and phone number (contact picker preferred
       over typing).
    3. System checks whether a platform supplier matches the phone
       number.
    4a. MATCH FOUND:
        business_suppliers created with supplier_id set,
        is_on_platform = true. Orders will flow digitally immediately.
    4b. NO MATCH:
        business_suppliers created with supplier_id NULL,
        is_on_platform = false. The business can still record orders
        against this supplier for its own records, and may send an
        invitation.
    5. Supplier appears in My Suppliers.

Rules:
    - A supplier can ALWAYS be added, whether or not they are on the
      platform. This is non-negotiable: requiring supplier presence would
      make business value dependent on supplier adoption (TP-019).
    - Duplicate detection is by phone number within a business.
    - No approval is required from the supplier for the business to keep
      its own record of the relationship.

================================================================================
6. INVITE SUPPLIER
================================================================================

    1. Business selects an off-platform supplier and taps Invite.
    2. Invitation sent (SMS/WhatsApp link or email, per available
       contact detail); invitation_sent_at recorded.
    3. If the supplier registers using that invitation:
        - Their new supplier account links to the inviting business's
          business_suppliers record.
        - supplier_id populated, is_on_platform set true.
        - Both parties notified.
        - Historical orders recorded locally against that supplier remain
          intact and become visible to the supplier from the link date
          forward only.

Rules:
    - Historical orders recorded before the supplier joined are the
      BUSINESS's records. They are not retroactively presented to the
      supplier as actionable orders -- doing so would surface stale
      obligations. They remain in the business's own history.
    - Invitations are rate-limited to prevent misuse.

================================================================================
7. PLACE ORDER
================================================================================

    1. Business selects a supplier from My Suppliers.
    2. Adds items:
        - Supplier Pro: selects from the supplier's catalogue.
        - Supplier Free / off-platform: free-text item names, quantities,
          and (optional) expected prices.
    3. Confirms delivery address and any note.
    4. Submits. Order created with status = pending and a
       client_generated_id.
    5. If the supplier is on-platform: supplier notified, order appears
       in their queue.
       If off-platform: order is recorded for the business's records
       only, status remains business-managed.

Rules:
    - One order targets exactly one supplier.
    - Item prices are captured at order time and never retroactively
      changed by later catalogue edits.
    - Orders may be created offline; they queue and sync (Section 14).

================================================================================
8. REPEAT ORDER
================================================================================

    1. Business opens a previous order and taps Repeat.
    2. A new draft order is pre-filled with the same supplier, items, and
       quantities.
    3. Business adjusts quantities if needed and submits.

Rationale: informal business ordering is highly repetitive. Re-entering
identical orders is the single largest avoidable friction in the product
(TP-059, TP-061).

================================================================================
9. ORDER LIFECYCLE (SUPPLIER SIDE)
================================================================================

    pending -> accepted -> in_fulfilment -> dispatched -> delivered ->
    completed

    Alternate terminal states: rejected, cancelled.

    Accept:    supplier accepts; accepted_at recorded; business notified.
    Reject:    supplier declines WITH a reason (out of stock, outside
               delivery area, minimum not met); business notified with the
               reason.
    Dispatch:  supplier marks dispatched; dispatched_at recorded.
    Deliver:   see Section 10.

Rules:
    - A rejection is a supplier-side decision and does NOT count against
      the business's trading history (Document 07).
    - Frequent rejection affects supplier reputation signals.
    - Only the supplier who owns the order may progress its status.

================================================================================
10. DELIVERY CONFIRMATION
================================================================================

    1. Either party marks the order delivered.
    2. delivery_confirmation recorded with timestamp, confirming user,
       and confirming role.
    3. Both parties notified.
    4. Order moves to delivered.

Rules:
    - Confirmation by either party is valid and recorded as such. Who
      confirmed is preserved, because that is precisely what resolves
      later disputes.
    - Delivery confirmation is separate from payment. An order may be
      delivered and unpaid, or paid and undelivered.

Problem solved: today delivery is confirmed verbally and disputes reduce
to memory. A timestamped record from a known party removes the most
common source of informal trade dispute.

================================================================================
11. PAYMENT EVENTS
================================================================================

    1. Supplier issues a payment reference for the order
       (reference_issued).
    2. Business pays the supplier DIRECTLY, outside TRUConnect, by
       whatever means the two parties already use.
    3. Business marks payment sent (payment_marked_sent).
    4. Supplier confirms receipt (payment_confirmed).
    5. Order moves to completed once delivered AND payment confirmed.

Rules (constitutional, non-negotiable):
    - TRUConnect NEVER holds, transfers, or escrows funds.
    - TRUConnect records THAT a payment occurred, not the payment itself.
    - Payment events are claims by each party, timestamped and
      attributed. The platform does not adjudicate disputes; it makes the
      record unambiguous.

================================================================================
12. LOAN BOOK LIFECYCLE
================================================================================

12.1 Record a Credit Sale
    1. Business selects or adds a customer (name; phone optional).
    2. Enters amount and date (defaults to today), optional description.
    3. credit_ledger_entry created; outstanding_amount = original_amount.

12.2 Record a Repayment
    1. Business opens the entry and records a payment amount.
    2. credit_ledger_payment created.
    3. outstanding_amount recalculated.
    4. If outstanding reaches zero: is_settled = true, settled_at
       recorded.

12.3 Rules
    - Partial repayment is fully supported; this is how informal credit
      actually behaves.
    - Repayments may exceed neither the outstanding amount nor be
      negative.
    - Entries may be corrected or removed (with audit trail) because
      manual entry errors are inevitable.
    - Loan Book entries may be created offline and queue for sync.
    - TRUConnect does not lend, does not collect, and does not charge
      interest. It replaces a paper book.

================================================================================
13. SUPPLIER TIER CHANGES
================================================================================

    1. Supplier upgrades (Free -> Pro, Pro -> Enterprise).
    2. A new supplier_tiers row is created with effective_from = now;
       the previous row's effective_until is set.
    3. Permissions are re-resolved; new capabilities become available
       immediately.
    4. On downgrade: previously created data (products, staff) is
       RETAINED but becomes read-only or hidden, never deleted.

Rationale: destroying a supplier's catalogue on downgrade would be a
severe and irreversible data loss for a paying-then-lapsed customer.

================================================================================
14. OFFLINE SYNC
================================================================================

    1. User performs an action while offline (place order, record credit
       sale, record repayment, confirm delivery).
    2. The action is stored locally with a client_generated_id and
       displayed with SyncStatusBadge = pending_sync.
    3. On reconnect, queued actions are submitted via /sync/batch.
    4. Server processes each item:
        - Not seen before -> applied; result returned.
        - client_generated_id already seen -> duplicate; ORIGINAL result
          returned, no second record created.
        - Invalid -> rejected with a reason.
    5. Client updates each item to synced or sync_failed.

Rules:
    - Duplicate submission must NEVER create a second record. A retried
      order sync becoming two orders becomes two real deliveries.
    - The user must always be able to distinguish confirmed from pending
      actions.
    - Sync is automatic and background; it never requires user action.
    - Failed items remain visible and retryable rather than silently
      disappearing.

================================================================================
15. TRUST RECALCULATION TRIGGERS
================================================================================

Recalculation of derived profiles and scores (Document 07) is triggered
asynchronously by:

    - Order placed, accepted, rejected, dispatched, delivered, completed,
      cancelled
    - Delivery confirmed
    - Payment confirmed
    - New supplier relationship established (first completed order)
    - Verification status change
    - Loan Book settlement patterns (aggregate behaviour only)

Recalculation never blocks the user-facing request.

================================================================================
16. NOTIFICATION RULES
================================================================================

    - Order status changes notify the counterparty immediately.
    - Delivery and payment confirmations notify both parties.
    - Supplier invitations notify the inviting business on acceptance.
    - Verification outcomes notify immediately.
    - Loan Book reminders are business-local and never sent to the
      customer by TRUConnect. The platform does not contact a business's
      customers on its behalf.

================================================================================
17. DATA INTEGRITY RULES
================================================================================

    - Soft delete for businesses, suppliers, orders, verification
      requests, and Loan Book entries.
    - Account deactivation hides the account from discovery but preserves
      historical trade records for both counterparties.
    - Order history is immutable once terminal, except for post-delivery
      metadata.
    - A business's Loan Book is private to that business. It is never
      exposed to suppliers, other businesses, or (in TRUConnect) any
      third party.
