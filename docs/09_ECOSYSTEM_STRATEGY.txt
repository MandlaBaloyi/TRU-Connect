--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT CHANGED IN THIS VERSION, AND WHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Previous versions of this document were built around "Business
Conversations" -- public Q&A beneath supplier products, presented as a
superior alternative to star ratings.

That entire strategy has been REMOVED.

The reasoning was sound for a marketplace: if a business is evaluating a
supplier it has never dealt with, seeing real questions and answers is
far more informative than a 4.7-star average. But the premise was wrong.

Businesses are not evaluating unknown suppliers. They already know their
suppliers. They have known them for years. They do not need public Q&A to
assess whether a supplier is reliable -- they have direct experience.

Business Questions solved a discovery problem that TRUConnect does not
have. It is removed rather than repurposed, because a feature justified
by a false premise should not survive by finding a new rationale.

================================================================================
1. CORE STRATEGIC POSITION
================================================================================

TRUConnect is digital trade infrastructure.

    It does not create trading relationships.
    It digitizes the ones that already exist.

The strongest network in the informal economy is already built: millions
of businesses already trade with suppliers they already trust (TP-056).
Nobody needs to be introduced. What is missing is the infrastructure to
run those relationships efficiently and make them visible.

================================================================================
2. WHY NOT A MARKETPLACE
================================================================================

A marketplace optimises for discovery: matching buyers with sellers they
do not yet know, then extracting a fee from the transaction.

That model fails here for four reasons:

    1. The discovery problem is largely imaginary. A spaza shop owner
       knows exactly which wholesaler they buy from. Discovery is not
       their pain.

    2. Marketplaces disintermediate relationships. TRUConnect's entire
       value depends on STRENGTHENING existing relationships. Inserting
       the platform between long-standing trading partners would destroy
       the trust the platform exists to measure.

    3. Marketplace economics require transaction fees, which require
       being in the payment path, which would make TRUConnect a money
       holder. The Constitution forbids this.

    4. Marketplace metrics (GMV, take rate) would drive the product
       toward transaction volume rather than relationship depth --
       exactly the wrong optimisation target for building trust.

TRUConnect is infrastructure, not a marketplace (TP-057). This
distinction governs every future product decision.

================================================================================
3. WHO WE ACTUALLY COMPETE WITH
================================================================================

Not Amazon. Not Takealot. Not Alibaba.

The real competition (TP-054):
    WhatsApp. Phone calls. SMS. Paper order books. Handwritten invoices.
    Excel spreadsheets. Memory.

These are the incumbent operational tools of millions of businesses.
They are free, universally understood, and already work "well enough".

This sets a demanding bar. To displace WhatsApp for placing an order,
TRUConnect must be FASTER than sending a WhatsApp message -- not merely
better structured. This is why repeat-order, contact-picker supplier
entry, and minimal-typing design are strategic requirements rather than
UX preferences (TP-059, TP-061).

We compete against operational friction, not against other platforms.

================================================================================
4. WHY BUSINESSES COME FIRST
================================================================================

The ecosystem is deliberately sequenced (TP-086, TP-019):

    Business Free -> Supplier Free -> Supplier Pro ->
    Supplier Enterprise -> Platform Services

Businesses first, because:
    - They experience the sharpest operational pain.
    - They are the reason suppliers will eventually join. A supplier
      joins because their customers are already there; the reverse does
      not happen.
    - They are the ultimate beneficiary of the entire ecosystem (TP-086).

This creates a hard design constraint: a business must get real value
with ZERO suppliers on the platform. That constraint is why
business_suppliers.supplier_id is nullable, why orders can be recorded
against off-platform suppliers, and why the Loan Book (which involves no
supplier at all) is a Stage 1 feature.

================================================================================
5. WHY THE LOAN BOOK MATTERS STRATEGICALLY
================================================================================

The Loan Book looks like a small utility. It is strategically important
for three reasons:

    1. It delivers value with zero network dependency. No supplier, no
       counterparty, no connectivity required. It works on day one for a
       business that is completely alone on the platform.

    2. It addresses one of the most universally reported operational
       pains in informal retail. Credit sales recorded on paper are lost,
       damaged, miscounted, and disputed constantly.

    3. It creates a daily-use habit. A business touches its credit book
       every day; it may only order stock weekly. Daily use is what makes
       TRUConnect part of the operational routine rather than an
       occasional tool.

It also produces behavioural signal -- but that is a consequence, never
the justification. It exists because it solves a real problem today
(TP-049).

================================================================================
6. WHY SUPPLIER TIERS
================================================================================

Freemium is a constitutional principle (TP-046): the ecosystem grows
because participation is easy.

    Supplier Free      Removes the barrier to supplier participation
                       entirely. Suppliers do not "buy in"; they simply
                       start receiving their existing customers' orders
                       digitally.

    Supplier Pro       Revenue earned by solving harder problems:
                       catalogue management, staff, reporting, web
                       access. A supplier pays because managing 200
                       products across 3 staff on a phone is genuinely
                       painful.

    Supplier Enterprise Revenue from operational complexity at scale:
                       teams, ERP integration, APIs.

Essential functionality -- receiving and fulfilling orders -- is never
paywalled. The platform monetises additional value created on top of
trusted infrastructure, never trust itself (TP-047).

================================================================================
7. WHY RELATIONSHIPS, NOT TRANSACTIONS
================================================================================

The business-supplier relationship is the fundamental unit of the
platform, not the individual order.

    - Relationships are far harder to fabricate than transactions.
    - Sustained relationships are far more predictive of continued
      reliable behaviour.
    - Relationship depth is what makes the data valuable to any future
      assessment.

A business with three multi-year supplier relationships may represent a
stronger trust profile than one with thirty scattered one-off purchases.
This is why the trust schema is organised around relationships and why
repeat-order behaviour is weighted heavily (Document 07).

================================================================================
8. WHY BILATERAL CONFIRMATION IS THE MOAT
================================================================================

Anyone can build an app where a business types in its own sales figures.
That data is worth almost nothing, because it is unverifiable.

TRUConnect's data is different: an order exists because a business placed
it AND a supplier accepted it. A delivery exists because a known party
confirmed it. A payment exists because both sides acknowledged it.

This bilateral confirmation is the platform's genuine competitive
advantage. It cannot be replicated by a competitor's feature release --
only by years of accumulated real trading activity between real parties
(TP-058).

Every design decision that increases the proportion of bilaterally
confirmed activity strengthens the moat. Every decision that increases
self-reported-only data weakens it.

================================================================================
9. WHY TRUCONNECT NEVER TOUCHES MONEY
================================================================================

Payments flow directly:

    Customer -> Supplier

TRUConnect records the event. It never holds, transfers, or escrows
funds.

Strategic reasons beyond the constitutional requirement:
    - Holding funds triggers financial regulation that would slow the
      platform enormously at exactly the stage it needs to move fast.
    - It would change the trust relationship: businesses would evaluate
      TRUConnect as a financial institution rather than an operational
      tool.
    - It is unnecessary. The payment EVENT is what produces trust signal;
      custody of the money adds risk without adding signal.

TRUWallet may eventually exist as a separate TruePoint product. That is a
different product, at a later stage, with a different regulatory posture.

================================================================================
10. HOW TRUST ACCUMULATES
================================================================================

    1. Verification establishes baseline identity trust.
    2. Recorded supplier relationships establish commercial context.
    3. Completed, bilaterally confirmed orders establish transactional
       trust.
    4. Repeat orders within the same relationship establish durable
       trust.
    5. Consistent delivery and payment confirmation reinforce it over
       time.

No step can be skipped, and no step can be purchased. This is what makes
the resulting record credible to any future assessor (TP-053).

================================================================================
11. HOW TRUCONNECT BECOMES INFRASTRUCTURE
================================================================================

As adoption compounds, TRUConnect stops being an app and becomes the
operational layer of a large network of verified businesses, verified
suppliers, and confirmed trade between them.

At that point it becomes possible -- for the first time -- to answer
questions that were previously unanswerable about the informal economy:
who trades consistently, with whom, and how reliably.

That capability is what later TruePoint products (TRUScore, TRUWallet,
TRUPayLater) are built upon. TRUConnect's strategic job is to build that
foundation properly, and to resist becoming any of those things itself
(TP-008, TP-084).

================================================================================
12. THE TRUEPOINT DIFFERENCE
================================================================================

    Traditional Marketplace
        Goal            Facilitate discovery and transactions
        Primary Asset   Product listings
        Primary Metric  GMV / transaction volume
        Competitor      Other marketplaces
        Relationship    Platform intermediates between strangers

    TRUConnect
        Goal            Digitize existing trade relationships
        Primary Asset   Bilaterally confirmed trading records
        Primary Metric  Relationship depth, confirmed activity, retention
        Competitor      WhatsApp, paper, phone calls, memory
        Relationship    Platform strengthens relationships it did not
                        create

================================================================================
13. THE GOVERNING TEST
================================================================================

Every proposed feature must pass:

    1. What business problem does this solve TODAY?
    2. Does it reduce phone calls, WhatsApp messages, manual records, or
       human error?
    3. Does it work if the counterparty is not on the platform?
       (If not -- is that acceptable at this stage?)
    4. Does it work offline, or degrade gracefully?
    5. Does it strengthen an EXISTING relationship rather than
       intermediate it?
    6. Does it increase bilaterally confirmed activity?
    7. Could it be simpler?

A feature that fails question 1 does not belong in TRUConnect, regardless
of how attractive it appears.
