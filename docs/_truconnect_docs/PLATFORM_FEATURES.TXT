# TRU Connect – Independent Business Features (Offline-First Foundation)

## Purpose

These features ensure that every informal business can use TRU Connect from day one, regardless of whether suppliers are available on the platform.

The supplier network remains a core part of the long-term vision, but it is **not a requirement** for businesses to benefit from TRU Connect.

Every feature below is designed to help informal businesses solve real daily operational problems while continuously building trusted business data for future supplier integration and TruePoint financing.

---

# 1. 📒 Loan Book

### Problem

Many informal businesses sell products on credit using paper notebooks. These notebooks are easily lost, damaged, or difficult to manage, making it hard to track who owes money and when repayments are due.

### Solution

A simple digital Loan Book that works exactly like the traditional notebook while making repayments, balances, reminders, and transaction history easier to manage.

### Design Principles

* Less than 5 taps
* Under 30 seconds
* Works completely offline
* Automatic background sync
* Generates trusted repayment history

---

# 2. ⚡ Fast Queue

### Problem

Busy businesses such as Kota shops, food vendors, cafés, and takeaways struggle to remember customer orders during peak trading hours, leading to confusion, forgotten orders, and slower service.

### Solution

A lightweight digital order queue that assigns each order a number and tracks its status from creation to collection.

### Design Principles

* Less than 5 taps
* Under 30 seconds
* Works completely offline
* Faster than writing on paper
* Automatic background sync

---

# 3. 💰 Cash Counter

### Problem

At the end of the trading day, many business owners are unsure whether the cash they have matches the business they conducted throughout the day.

### Solution

A simple opening and closing cash checker that compares expected cash against actual cash using recorded transactions.

### Design Principles

* Extremely simple workflow
* Less than 5 taps
* Under 30 seconds
* Works offline
* No accounting knowledge required

---

# 4. 📦 Restock Reminder

### Problem

Business owners often forget when to restock frequently purchased products, resulting in stock shortages and missed sales.

### Solution

Instead of requiring complicated inventory management, TRU Connect learns business purchasing habits and reminds owners when it is time to restock commonly purchased products.

### Design Principles

* No inventory counting
* No manual stock management
* Offline capable
* Automatic learning over time
* Simple Yes/No reminders

---

# 5. 🧾 Digital Receipt

### Problem

Customers often request proof of payment or proof of purchase, while paper receipts are easily lost.

### Solution

Generate simple digital receipts for Loan Book transactions and Fast Queue orders that can be shared whenever internet becomes available.

### Design Principles

* One-tap receipt generation
* Supports customer trust
* Works offline
* Automatically syncs later
* Creates verifiable transaction history

---

# 6. 📅 Money Due Today

### Problem

Business owners often forget which customers promised to pay today, delaying collections and affecting cash flow.

### Solution

Automatically display all repayments due today, allowing business owners to quickly record payments and update balances.

### Design Principles

* Automatic reminders
* One-tap repayment recording
* Offline capable
* Faster debt collection
* Improves cash flow management

---

# 7. 📊 Daily Snapshot

### Problem

Many informal businesses finish trading without understanding what happened during the day.

### Solution

A simple daily summary showing the most important information without requiring reports or accounting knowledge.

### Example

* Orders Completed
* Money Collected
* Outstanding Loans
* Customers Served

Everything is calculated automatically.

### Design Principles

* No charts required
* No accounting knowledge
* Under 30 seconds
* Works offline
* Simple business overview

---

# Offline-First Architecture

Every core business feature is designed to operate without an internet connection.

When connectivity is unavailable:

* Business continues normally.
* Every action is saved securely on the device.
* No information is lost.
* No user interaction is required.

When connectivity returns:

* Data automatically synchronizes with the TRU Connect platform.
* No manual syncing.
* No additional login required.
* No interruption to business operations.

---

# Product Design Principles

Every TRU Connect feature must satisfy the following requirements:

* Solves a real daily business problem.
* Requires fewer than 5 taps.
* Takes less than 30 seconds.
* Works without internet.
* Simpler than using a paper notebook.
* Requires minimal typing.
* Automatically calculates insights instead of asking users to perform calculations.
* Generates trusted business data in the background.

---

# Platform Vision

These features do **not** replace the TRU Connect supplier ecosystem.

Instead, they ensure that every informal business can immediately benefit from the platform, regardless of location, connectivity, or supplier availability.

As supplier adoption grows, businesses already using TRU Connect can seamlessly access supplier services without changing how they run their businesses.

This approach removes adoption barriers while preserving the long-term vision of connecting informal businesses, suppliers, and TruePoint financing into one integrated ecosystem.

# TRU Connect – Supplier Strategy & Platform Philosophy

## Vision

TRU Connect is **not** a traditional e-commerce marketplace.

The platform is designed around how the informal economy already operates, rather than trying to change existing buying behaviour.

The goal is not to help businesses discover thousands of new suppliers.

The goal is to make existing supplier-business relationships faster, simpler, and more reliable.

---

# Understanding the Informal Economy

Unlike traditional retail or online shopping platforms, informal businesses rarely spend time browsing hundreds of products or comparing dozens of suppliers.

Most informal businesses already have trusted suppliers.

A spaza shop owner usually buys bread from the same bread distributor.

A kota shop already knows where they buy chips, polony, drinks, bread, and other daily stock.

Relationships already exist.

Communication is the problem.

Operations are the problem.

Trust and record keeping are the problem.

TRU Connect exists to solve those problems.

---

# The Current Reality

Today, businesses place orders using:

* Phone calls
* WhatsApp messages
* SMS
* Facebook Messenger

Although these methods are familiar, they create operational challenges.

Common problems include:

* Suppliers forgetting customer orders.
* Incorrect quantities being delivered.
* Orders getting buried inside WhatsApp conversations.
* No structured order history.
* No delivery tracking.
* No confirmation that an order was received.
* No business insights.
* No trusted trading records.

The relationship already exists.

The workflow is broken.

---

# TRU Connect Philosophy

TRU Connect does not replace supplier relationships.

TRU Connect digitizes existing supplier relationships.

The platform removes unnecessary communication while preserving trust between businesses and suppliers.

Instead of replacing how people trade, TRU Connect improves how they manage those trades.

---

# Relationship-First Marketplace

Traditional marketplaces focus on product discovery.

TRU Connect focuses on relationship management.

Instead of showing endless product listings and infinite scrolling, businesses build a list of trusted suppliers.

Example:

My Suppliers

⭐ ABC Bread Distributor

⭐ Coca-Cola Distributor

⭐ Local Dairy Supplier

⭐ Township Cash & Carry

Businesses simply select a supplier they already trust and place an order.

No browsing.

No endless searching.

No unnecessary complexity.

---

# Supplier Ordering Experience

Ordering stock should take less than thirty seconds.

Typical workflow:

Open TRU Connect

↓

My Suppliers

↓

Select Supplier

↓

Buy Again

↓

Adjust Quantity (Optional)

↓

Confirm Order

The objective is to replace phone calls and WhatsApp conversations with a structured order that both parties can immediately trust.

---

# Supplier Dashboard

The supplier dashboard is not designed as a complex ERP system.

Instead, it answers one simple question:

"What do I need to deliver today?"

Example:

Good Morning

Today's Orders

15 Orders Received

1,240 Bread

180 Milk

92 Cold Drink Crates

Delivery Route Ready

Instead of spending the morning searching through WhatsApp conversations and handwritten notes, suppliers immediately know what needs to be packed and delivered.

---

# Intelligent Delivery Planning

As orders are received, TRU Connect automatically groups deliveries by location.

Example:

Today's Route

1. Mankweng

2. Turfloop

3. Seshego

4. Polokwane CBD

5. Ga-Molepo

This reduces unnecessary travel while simplifying delivery planning.

The supplier focuses on deliveries instead of route planning.

---

# Hybrid Payments

TRU Connect does not force businesses to use digital payments.

Businesses continue paying the way they already operate.

Supported payment methods include:

* Cash
* EFT
* Card
* Credit (Loan Book)
* Future TRU Wallet Integration

The platform records how payment was made without forcing behavioural change.

---

# Offline-First Operations

Businesses can continue creating supplier orders even without internet connectivity.

Orders are saved securely on the device.

When connectivity returns, orders automatically synchronize with the supplier.

Business operations never stop because of poor network coverage.

---

# Product Principles

Every supplier feature must satisfy the following principles:

* Solve a real operational problem.
* Less than five taps.
* Less than thirty seconds.
* Simpler than making a phone call.
