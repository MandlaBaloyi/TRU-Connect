================================================================================
1. CORE OBJECTIVE
================================================================================

TRU Connect is not designed to become another online marketplace.

Its purpose is to help businesses discover trusted suppliers, build trusted
business relationships, and quietly create the business data needed to
unlock future financial opportunities.

Buying and selling products is only one feature of a much larger ecosystem.
Every feature decision in this document should be evaluated against this
objective before being adopted.

================================================================================
2. CORE PHILOSOPHY: RELATIONSHIPS OVER TRANSACTIONS
================================================================================

Traditional marketplaces focus on transactions. TRU Connect focuses on
relationships.

Every interaction between businesses and suppliers contributes to building
a trusted digital business ecosystem, not merely to closing a sale. This
philosophy shapes every decision documented below.

================================================================================
3. WHY BUSINESS QUESTIONS INSTEAD OF REVIEWS
================================================================================

Instead of traditional product reviews or private messaging, TRU Connect
introduces Business Conversations: businesses ask questions directly
beneath supplier products (availability, delivery locations and times,
bulk pricing, minimum order quantities, packaging, specifications, general
business enquiries). Supplier responses remain attached to the product,
so future businesses benefit from previously answered questions.

Why this matters:
    - Star ratings are easily manipulated and rarely reflect real
      commercial behaviour. A rating of "4.7 stars" tells a business
      almost nothing actionable.
    - Business Questions create transparency instead: businesses can see
      exactly what was asked, how the supplier responded, and infer
      delivery capability, pricing policy, service quality, and business
      professionalism directly from real exchanges.
    - Rather than isolated, one-off conversations, every discussion
      becomes shared business knowledge — a growing Business Knowledge
      Base per supplier (questions, responses, delivery/packaging/
      availability/bulk-order/region information, effectively a living
      FAQ), reducing repetitive enquiries and creating long-term value for
      both suppliers and future customers.
    - Trust becomes visible through communication rather than ratings
      alone.

================================================================================
4. WHY SUPPLIER PROFILES ARE PUBLIC
================================================================================

Supplier profiles remain publicly visible, showing profile information,
verification status, products, business information, Business Questions,
Business Updates, and customer statistics.

Why this matters:
    - Public visibility increases transparency and encourages trust — a
      business considering a new supplier should be able to fully assess
      that supplier's track record before ever placing an order.
    - Suppliers benefit from public profiles as a discovery and reputation-
      building channel, similar to a professional storefront, reinforcing
      their standalone incentive to participate on the platform.

================================================================================
5. WHY BUSINESS PROFILES ARE PROTECTED
================================================================================

Business customers remain largely private. Rather than exposing detailed
business information, customer identities are limited to a generic
"Verified Business" or a partially identified label such as "ABC Store
(Verified)," without revealing unnecessary commercial information.

Why this matters:
    - Protecting customer privacy helps maintain trust while reducing
      unnecessary competitive exposure — a small business should not have
      to worry that its sourcing relationships or order volumes are
      visible to competitors.
    - This asymmetry (public suppliers, private businesses) is
      intentional: suppliers are B2B storefronts seeking visibility;
      businesses are the platform's primary users seeking protection and
      utility, not exposure.

================================================================================
6. WHY RELATIONSHIPS MATTER
================================================================================

A specific, ongoing relationship between one business and one supplier
(Document 04, Section 16; Document 07, Section 3) is treated as the
fundamental unit of trust on TRU Connect — more informative than any single
transaction.

Why this matters:
    - Relationships are harder to fabricate than isolated transactions and
      are far more predictive of continued reliable behaviour.
    - A business with deep, sustained relationships with a small number of
      suppliers may represent a stronger trust profile than one with many
      shallow, one-off purchases.
    - This is why Relationship Health Score, Repeat Supplier Rate, and
      Repeat Customer Rate are first-class, prominently displayed metrics
      throughout the product (Documents 03 and 07), rather than secondary
      statistics.

================================================================================
7. WHY REPEAT ORDERS MATTER
================================================================================

Repeat orders between the same business-supplier pair are weighted more
heavily than first-time orders in all trust calculations (Document 07,
Section 4).

Why this matters:
    - A business that orders from the same supplier repeatedly is
      demonstrating sustained satisfaction and reliability, a far stronger
      signal than a single completed transaction.
    - Repeat behaviour is also difficult to fake at scale, making it a
      valuable, resilient signal for future risk and credit models
      (Phase 3, Document 08).

================================================================================
8. WHY BUSINESS DATA MATTERS
================================================================================

Every activity performed within TRU Connect — searching, viewing profiles
and products, asking questions, receiving responses, bookmarking,
following, receiving enquiries, quotations, placing and completing orders,
repeat purchases, delivery performance, payment behaviour, product
engagement, and verification — contributes to a richer digital business
profile.

None of these are "just marketplace features." Each interaction represents
verified commercial behaviour, and collectively they form the foundation
for future business insights, AI-powered recommendations, credit risk
assessment, supplier financing, trade credit, working capital, and
financial partnerships (Document 08, Phases 2–4).

Businesses are never required to manually build their financial profile.
It grows naturally through everyday business activity — this is the direct
product expression of the "invisible data collection" principle in
Document 00.

================================================================================
9. HOW TRUST IS BUILT
================================================================================

Trust on TRU Connect accumulates through a layered process:

    1. Verification establishes a baseline identity trust signal.
    2. Ordinary activity (search, viewing, questions) establishes early
       engagement signals.
    3. Completed orders establish transactional trust.
    4. Repeat orders and sustained relationships establish durable,
       high-confidence trust.
    5. Consistent payment behaviour and low dispute/cancellation rates
       reinforce and protect that trust over time.

This layered accumulation (detailed technically in Document 07) is what
allows TRU Connect to eventually support credit decisioning without relying
on traditional collateral, paperwork, or credit bureau history that
excludes informal businesses today.

================================================================================
10. HOW COMMERCIAL RELATIONSHIPS BECOME BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE
================================================================================

The Data and Trust Engine (Document 07) systematically converts the
day-to-day commercial relationships described above into structured,
queryable business intelligence: the Digital Business Profile, the
Supplier Profile, Relationship Health Scores, and — eventually — Business
Health Scores and formal risk models.

This is the mechanism by which "relationships" (a qualitative, human
concept) become "business intelligence" (a quantitative, systematic asset)
without requiring the business or supplier to do anything beyond using the
platform as intended.

================================================================================
11. HOW BUSINESS ACTIVITY CREATES FINANCIAL READINESS
================================================================================

As described in Document 07, Section 13, "Credit Readiness" is a
cumulative output of ordinary platform activity crossing defined,
configurable thresholds — not a separate application process.

This ensures the platform's financial products (Phase 3, Document 08) are
available first to those businesses and suppliers who have already
demonstrated reliability through real behaviour, directly operationalising
the "Trust Before Lending" principle from Document 00.

================================================================================
12. HOW TRU CONNECT EVOLVES INTO DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE
================================================================================

As adoption grows, the accumulated network of verified businesses,
verified suppliers, sustained relationships, and behavioural data
(Document 07) evolves TRU Connect from a single application into shared
infrastructure: a trust and data layer that other participants — financial
institutions, logistics partners, insurers — can eventually build upon via
Partner APIs (Phase 4, Document 08).

================================================================================
13. HOW TRUEPOINT GAINS COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGE
================================================================================

TruePoint's competitive advantage does not come from being a marketplace
with more listings or lower prices than competitors. It comes from owning
a unique, defensible asset: a growing base of verified, trusted commercial
relationships and behavioural data that is extremely difficult for a
generic marketplace to replicate after the fact, because it can only be
built through sustained real usage over time.

================================================================================
14. THE TRUEPOINT DIFFERENCE (SUMMARY COMPARISON)
================================================================================

    Traditional Marketplace
        Goal:            Facilitate buying and selling.
        Primary Asset:   Products.
        Primary Metric:  Transactions.

    TRU Connect Ecosystem
        Goal:            Build trusted business relationships and digital
                         business infrastructure.
        Primary Asset:   Trusted commercial data.
        Primary Metric:  Business relationships, verified activity, and
                         long-term trust.

================================================================================
15. GUIDING PRINCIPLE
================================================================================

Every search. Every enquiry. Every supplier relationship. Every order.
Every business conversation. Every repeat purchase. Every verified
interaction.

Quietly builds the trusted digital business ecosystem that powers the
future of TruePoint.

Any proposed feature for TRU Connect should be evaluated against this
principle: does it deepen a genuine business-supplier relationship and
contribute honestly to trusted commercial data, or does it merely optimise
for a one-off transaction? Features that serve the former should be
prioritised; features that only serve the latter should be reconsidered.
