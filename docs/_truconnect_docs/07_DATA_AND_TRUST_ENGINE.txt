transformed into structured, trusted business intelligence — the platform's
core long-term asset.

================================================================================
1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Every interaction on TRU Connect — a search, a question, an order, a
delivery, a repeat purchase — is treated as a signal. These signals are
continuously aggregated into two outward-facing profiles:

    - The Digital Business Profile (for businesses)
    - The Supplier Profile / Business Performance profile (for suppliers)

Neither profile is manually completed by the user. Both are entirely
derived from observed platform activity, recalculated asynchronously as
described in Document 06, Sections 15–17.

================================================================================
2. DIGITAL BUSINESS PROFILE
================================================================================

Displayed fields and their derivation:

    Business Since           — registered_since date from Businesses table.
    Orders                    — count of completed orders (rejected/
                                cancelled orders excluded).
    Suppliers                — count of distinct suppliers with at least
                                one completed order (i.e. established
                                Relationships, Document 04 Section 16).
    Average Order            — mean total_amount across completed orders.
    Largest Order             — maximum total_amount across completed
                                orders.
    Repeat Supplier Rate      — percentage of suppliers with more than one
                                completed order, out of total suppliers
                                engaged.
    Average Payment Time      — mean number of days between order
                                acceptance and payment confirmation (Phase
                                1: proxied by order completion time in the
                                absence of a payments module; refined once
                                Phase 2 payments/wallet data is available).
    Business Growth           — percentage change in order volume and/or
                                value over a rolling period (e.g. quarter-
                                over-quarter).
    Risk Level                — a categorical (low/medium/high) output
                                derived from the signals in Section 4,
                                described further in Section 6.

================================================================================
3. SUPPLIER RELATIONSHIPS & RELATIONSHIP HEALTH
================================================================================

Each distinct business-supplier pair accrues its own relationship record
(Document 04, Section 16), tracking:

    - first_interaction_at / last_interaction_at
    - total_orders / total_order_value
    - relationship_health_score

Relationship Health Score is a composite signal reflecting:
    - Frequency of orders between the pair.
    - Recency of the last interaction (a relationship that has gone quiet
      contributes less to ongoing trust signals than an active one).
    - Order completion rate within the pair (completed vs. cancelled/
      rejected).
    - Responsiveness of Business Questions exchanged between the pair, if
      any.

Relationships are the fundamental unit of trust in TRU Connect, distinct
from and more informative than isolated transactions (see Document 09,
"Why Relationships Matter").

================================================================================
4. REPEAT ORDERS
================================================================================

Repeat orders — multiple completed orders between the same business and
supplier — are weighted more heavily than first-time orders in trust
calculations, because they represent a demonstrated, ongoing willingness to
transact again, which is a stronger signal of satisfaction and reliability
than a single transaction.

Repeat Supplier Rate (Business Profile) and Repeat Customer Rate (Supplier
Profile) are both first-class, prominently displayed metrics for this
reason.

================================================================================
5. PRODUCT VIEWS
================================================================================

Product view events (Document 06, Section 6) contribute to:
    - "Recently Viewed" personalisation for the viewing business.
    - Product and supplier popularity ranking (aggregate, anonymised).
    - Early-stage interest signals that may precede an order (used for
      future recommendation-engine training data, Phase 2+).

Product views alone do not materially affect a business's Digital Business
Profile risk/trust fields; they are primarily a discovery and
recommendation signal, not a creditworthiness signal.

================================================================================
6. BUSINESS QUESTIONS & SUPPLIER RESPONSES AS TRUST SIGNALS
================================================================================

Business Questions and Supplier Responses (see Document 09 for the product
rationale) generate two distinct signal streams:

6.1 For the Business (asker)
    - Engagement signal: businesses that actively ask questions before
      ordering demonstrate deliberate, considered purchasing behaviour, a
      mild positive input to overall engagement scoring.

6.2 For the Supplier (responder)
    - average_response_time_minutes — time between question submission
      and response submission.
    - response_rate_percentage — proportion of received questions that
      receive an official response at all.
    - These two metrics feed directly into the Supplier Profile and are
      surfaced publicly, since responsiveness is one of the strongest
      available proxies for supplier reliability in the absence of
      traditional reviews (Document 09).

================================================================================
7. ORDER BEHAVIOUR
================================================================================

Order-level behavioural signals tracked per business:
    - Order frequency and volume trend over time.
    - Distribution of orders across distinct suppliers (concentration vs.
      diversification of sourcing).
    - Cancellation rate (business-initiated cancellations, distinct from
      supplier rejections).
    - Value consistency (variance in order size over time).

Order-level behavioural signals tracked per supplier:
    - Acceptance rate of incoming orders.
    - Fulfilment completion rate.
    - Time from acceptance to completion.

================================================================================
8. RISK SIGNALS & TRUST SIGNALS
================================================================================

The platform distinguishes conceptually between:

    Trust Signals — evidence of positive, reliable commercial behaviour
    (repeat orders, verification, responsiveness, consistent payment
    behaviour, longevity on the platform).

    Risk Signals — evidence that may indicate unreliability or elevated
    risk (frequent cancellations, disputed orders, lapsed verification,
    inconsistent or declining activity, unusually rapid changes in order
    volume that do not correspond to a verified business event).

Both signal types feed into the Risk Level field on the Digital Business
Profile and, in future phases, into more formal risk-scoring models used
for credit and trade finance decisioning (Phase 3, see Document 08).

================================================================================
9. AI FEATURE GENERATION (FORWARD-LOOKING)
================================================================================

As platform activity volume grows, raw signals described above become
inputs (features) for machine learning models supporting:
    - Supplier and product recommendations personalised to a business's
      order history and category.
    - Early identification of at-risk relationships (e.g. a previously
      active relationship going quiet) for potential re-engagement
      prompts.
    - Business Health Score refinement beyond the initial rule-based
      calculation described in this document.
    - Future credit risk models (Phase 3), trained on the accumulated
      Trust and Risk Signal history described above.

Phase 1 implementation should favour transparent, rule-based calculations
(as described in Sections 2–8) over opaque ML models, both for simplicity
of initial build and for explainability of any early risk indicators shown
to users or, later, to financial partners.

================================================================================
10. BUSINESS HEALTH SCORE
================================================================================

The Business Health Score (referenced in the Digital Business Profile as
"Business Growth" and "Risk Level," and expandable in future UI iterations
into a single composite score) is calculated from a weighted combination
of:

    - Order volume and value trend (recency-weighted)
    - Repeat Supplier Rate
    - Verification status
    - Average Payment Time
    - Relationship Health Scores across all supplier relationships
    - Absence of negative Risk Signals (Section 8)

The precise weighting formula should be treated as a configurable,
versioned parameter set (not hard-coded), since it is expected to be
refined as more platform data becomes available and as Phase 3 credit
products introduce more rigorous validation requirements.

================================================================================
11. SUPPLIER REPUTATION
================================================================================

Mirroring the Business Health Score, supplier reputation is derived from:
    - Businesses Served (count of distinct businesses with completed
      orders)
    - Orders Completed
    - Repeat Customer Rate
    - Average Response Time / Response Rate
    - Verification Status
    - Active Since (longevity)

This composite reputation is what replaces traditional star ratings across
the platform (see Document 09 for full rationale), and is displayed via the
BusinessPerformanceCard component (Document 03).

================================================================================
12. COMMERCIAL RELATIONSHIPS AS THE UNIT OF TRUST
================================================================================

Where a traditional marketplace treats each transaction as an independent
event, TRU Connect's trust engine treats the business-supplier Relationship
(Document 04, Section 16) as the primary unit of analysis. A business with
fewer, deeper supplier relationships may in some contexts present a
stronger trust profile than one with many shallow, one-off transactions,
because sustained relationships are harder to fabricate and more
predictive of continued reliable behaviour.

================================================================================
13. FUTURE CREDIT READINESS
================================================================================

"Credit Readiness" is not a Phase 1 user-facing feature, but the trust
engine described in this document is explicitly designed to produce the
data needed for it in Phase 3:

    - A business or supplier is considered "credit-ready" in future
      phases once their accumulated Trust Signals cross defined,
      configurable thresholds (e.g. minimum order history length, minimum
      verification status, minimum relationship health across suppliers).
    - No business or supplier is ever required to manually assemble this
      readiness; it is a natural, cumulative output of ordinary platform
      use, consistent with the "Trust Before Lending" principle in
      Document 00.
    - When Phase 3 is reached, this document's signal set becomes the
      direct input specification for underwriting model design, avoiding
      the need to retrofit data collection after the fact.
