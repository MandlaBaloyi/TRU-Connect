                            TRU CONNECT
              BUSINESS RULES AND WORKFLOWS
                          Document 06 ⭐
================================================================================

Version: 1.0
Related Documents: 05_BACKEND_API_SPECIFICATION.txt,
                    07_DATA_AND_TRUST_ENGINE.txt

Purpose: Document every business workflow governing TRU Connect, so that
engineering, QA, and product teams share a single definition of expected
system behaviour.

================================================================================
1. BUSINESS REGISTRATION
================================================================================

    1. User selects "For Businesses" on the landing screen.
    2. User registers via email/password or Continue with Google.
    3. User provides basic business details: business name, category,
       location, contact number.
    4. Account is created with role = business, is_verified = false.
    5. User lands on the Business Home Dashboard.
    6. A Digital Business Profile record is initialised (zero-state) for
       the new business, ready to accrue data as described in Document 07.

Rules:
    - A single email or Google account may only be associated with one
      role (business or supplier). Switching roles requires a separate
      account.
    - Business registration does not require verification documents to be
      submitted immediately; verification may be completed at any point
      but certain actions (see Section 3) require it.

================================================================================
2. SUPPLIER REGISTRATION
================================================================================

    1. User selects "For Suppliers" on the landing screen.
    2. User registers via email/password or Continue with Google.
    3. User provides supplier details: supplier name, category, regions
       served, contact number.
    4. Account is created with role = supplier, is_verified = false.
    5. User lands on the Supplier Home Dashboard.
    6. A Supplier Profile record is initialised (zero-state).

Rules:
    - A supplier cannot publish products until at least basic profile
      information is complete (name, category, regions served).
    - As with businesses, verification is not mandatory at registration
      but gates certain higher-trust actions.

================================================================================
3. VERIFICATION
================================================================================

    1. User (business or supplier) submits verification documents via
       POST /verification/submit.
    2. Verification record is created with status = pending.
    3. Verification is reviewed (Phase 1: manual review process; future
       phases may introduce automated verification checks).
    4. On approval: status = approved, and is_verified flag on the parent
       Business/Supplier record is set to true. A Verification Badge
       becomes visible on the account's public profile and cards.
    5. On rejection: status = rejected, with reviewer notes retained
       internally (never shown to the end user beyond a generic
       "additional information required" message).

Rules:
    - Suppliers must reach approved verification status before their
      profile appears in "Featured Suppliers" listings (though they may
      still appear in general search/discovery as unverified).
    - Businesses are not required to verify to place orders in Phase 1,
      but verification directly contributes to a higher Business Health
      Score (Document 07) and may become a requirement for future credit
      products (Phase 3).

================================================================================
4. PRODUCT LISTING (SUPPLIER)
================================================================================

    1. Supplier creates a product: name, category, description, price,
       unit, minimum order quantity, stock status.
    2. Product is created with is_published = true by default (or false, if
       the supplier chooses to save as draft).
    3. Published products become visible in business-facing search,
       category browsing, and recommendations.
    4. Supplier may update stock status at any time (in_stock, low_stock,
       out_of_stock); out_of_stock products remain visible but are marked
       accordingly and excluded from "Popular Products" ranking.

Rules:
    - A product must belong to exactly one category.
    - Deleting a product does not hard-delete historical Order Items
      referencing it; the product record is soft-deleted / unpublished
      instead, preserving order history integrity.

================================================================================
5. SEARCHING SUPPLIERS (BUSINESS)
================================================================================

    1. Business enters a keyword and/or selects a category/location filter
       in the Suppliers tab.
    2. System returns matching suppliers, ranked by a combination of
       relevance, verification status, distance, and relationship
       signals (e.g. previously ordered-from suppliers may rank higher
       for that specific business — see Document 07).
    3. Business may further filter by "Verified only."

Rules:
    - Search and view events are logged as business activity, feeding the
      Digital Business Profile and recommendation systems (Document 07),
      but this is never surfaced to the user as a step in a "profile
      building" process — it is purely a background effect of normal use.

================================================================================
6. VIEWING PRODUCTS
================================================================================

    1. Business opens a supplier profile or search result and selects a
       product.
    2. Product Detail screen loads, including price, availability,
       minimum order quantity, and the attached Business Questions thread.
    3. View event is logged (contributes to "Recently Viewed" and supplier/
       product popularity signals).

================================================================================
7. BUSINESS QUESTIONS (ASK)
================================================================================

    1. Business submits a question beneath a product via the
       BusinessQuestionThread component.
    2. Question is stored, associated with the product and the asking
       business, and categorised (availability, delivery, pricing, bulk
       order, packaging, specifications, general).
    3. Supplier receives a notification of a new, unanswered question.
    4. Question and any future response remain permanently attached to the
       product, visible to all future viewers (see Document 09 rationale).

Rules:
    - A business may ask multiple questions on the same product.
    - Questions are not private messages; they are treated as shared,
      public product knowledge by design.

================================================================================
8. SUPPLIER RESPONSES
================================================================================

    1. Supplier views unanswered questions across their product catalogue
       (aggregated view) or on an individual product.
    2. Supplier submits a response.
    3. Responding business (and all future viewers of the product) can see
       the response attached beneath the original question.
    4. Response event updates the supplier's average_response_time_minutes
       and response_rate_percentage (Document 07).

Rules:
    - Only the supplier who owns the product may respond to a question on
      that product.
    - A question may only have one official Supplier Response (edits to an
      existing response are allowed; a second independent response is
      not, to avoid conflicting or duplicated answers).

================================================================================
9. BOOKMARK SUPPLIER
================================================================================

    1. Business selects "Save" or "Follow" on a supplier profile or
       Supplier Card.
    2. A Bookmark record is created linking the business and supplier.
    3. Bookmarked suppliers appear in the "Saved Suppliers" list and the
       business begins receiving notifications for that supplier's future
       Business Updates.

Rules:
    - Bookmarking is a one-directional signal; it does not require
      supplier approval and does not notify the supplier.

================================================================================
10. PLACE ORDER
================================================================================

    1. Business adds one or more products (with quantities, respecting
       each product's minimum order quantity) from a single supplier to an
       order.
    2. Business confirms delivery address and reviews order total.
    3. Business submits the order (POST /orders); order is created with
       status = pending.
    4. Supplier receives a notification of a new incoming order.

Rules:
    - An order may only contain products from a single supplier (no
      cross-supplier carts in Phase 1); ordering from multiple suppliers
      requires separate orders.
    - Order total is calculated at submission time using each product's
      current price and any applicable bulk pricing rules; this price is
      locked into the Order Item (unit_price_at_order_time) regardless of
      later price changes.

================================================================================
11. ACCEPT ORDER
================================================================================

    1. Supplier reviews an incoming pending order.
    2. Supplier accepts the order (PUT /orders/{id}/accept).
    3. Order status transitions to accepted; accepted_at timestamp is
       recorded.
    4. Business receives a notification of acceptance.
    5. Order proceeds to in_fulfilment status once the supplier begins
       preparing/dispatching the order (implementation may combine this
       step with acceptance depending on supplier workflow needs).

================================================================================
12. REJECT ORDER
================================================================================

    1. Supplier declines an incoming pending order, providing a reason
       (e.g. out of stock, outside delivery area, minimum not met).
    2. Order status transitions to rejected.
    3. Business receives a notification including the rejection reason.

Rules:
    - A rejected order does not count against the business's order
      history in a negative way; it is excluded from repeat-order and
      trust calculations for that business (Document 07), since the
      failure to fulfil originates with the supplier's decision.
    - Frequent rejections by a supplier negatively affect that supplier's
      response_rate_percentage and overall Supplier Profile.

================================================================================
13. COMPLETE ORDER
================================================================================

    1. Supplier fulfils and delivers the order.
    2. Order status transitions to completed, either via explicit supplier
       action or system-triggered confirmation (e.g. delivery tracking
       reaching a "delivered" state, in later phases with more advanced
       logistics integration).
    3. completed_at timestamp is recorded.
    4. An Invoice becomes available for the order.
    5. Completion triggers recalculation of both the business's Digital
       Business Profile and the supplier's Supplier Profile statistics
       (Document 07).

================================================================================
14. ORDER HISTORY
================================================================================

    - Both business and supplier can view historical orders, filterable by
      status (current, pending, completed, cancelled/rejected).
    - Order history is immutable once an order reaches a terminal state
      (completed, cancelled, rejected); only delivery tracking metadata may
      continue to update post-completion for record-keeping purposes.

================================================================================
15. BUSINESS PROFILE UPDATES (DIGITAL BUSINESS PROFILE RECALCULATION)
================================================================================

Trigger events that cause recalculation of a business's Digital Business
Profile:
    - Order placed
    - Order completed
    - Order cancelled/rejected
    - New supplier relationship established (first order with a new
      supplier)
    - Verification status change

Recalculated fields: orders_count, suppliers_count, average_order_value,
largest_order_value, repeat_supplier_rate, average_payment_time_days,
business_growth_percentage, risk_level.

Full methodology is defined in Document 07.

================================================================================
16. SUPPLIER STATISTICS (SUPPLIER PROFILE RECALCULATION)
================================================================================

Trigger events:
    - Order received / accepted / rejected / completed
    - Business Question response submitted
    - New verification status change

Recalculated fields: businesses_served_count, orders_completed_count,
repeat_customer_rate, average_response_time_minutes,
response_rate_percentage.

================================================================================
17. TRUST UPDATES
================================================================================

    1. Any of the trigger events in Sections 15–16 causes an asynchronous
       recalculation job to run (rather than blocking the user-facing
       request/response cycle).
    2. Updated profile values are persisted to the Business Profile /
       Supplier Profile tables.
    3. Relationship-level statistics (Document 04, Section 16) are updated
       for the specific business-supplier pair involved.
    4. See Document 07 for the full set of signals feeding these
       calculations and their intended future use in risk assessment.

================================================================================
18. NOTIFICATION DELIVERY RULES
================================================================================

    - Order-related notifications (placed, accepted, rejected, completed)
      are delivered immediately/synchronously with the triggering event.
    - Business Question response notifications are delivered immediately
      to the original asking business.
    - Business Update notifications are delivered to all businesses that
      have bookmarked/followed the publishing supplier.
    - Verification status change notifications are delivered immediately
      upon review completion.

================================================================================
19. ACCOUNT & DATA INTEGRITY RULES
================================================================================

    - Soft-delete is used for Businesses, Suppliers, Orders, and
      Verification Records to preserve historical trust data (Document 04,
      Section 19).
    - A business or supplier may deactivate their own account; deactivation
      hides the profile from discovery but preserves historical order and
      relationship data for trust-engine integrity and any pending legal/
      financial record-keeping requirements.
