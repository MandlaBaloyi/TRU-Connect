CONSTITUTIONAL SEQUENCE (TP-023, TP-027, TP-083)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Operations -> Behaviour -> Trust -> Data -> Intelligence -> Finance

This order SHALL NEVER be reversed. TRUConnect operates entirely within
the first four stages. It produces trusted data. It does not produce
intelligence products or financial products.

================================================================================
1. OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Every ordinary action on TRUConnect is a signal: recording a supplier,
placing an order, confirming a delivery, acknowledging a payment,
settling a credit sale.

These accumulate into two derived profiles:
    - The Digital Business Profile (for businesses)
    - The Supplier Performance Profile (for suppliers)

Neither is manually completed by the user (TP-022). Both are recalculated
asynchronously from observed activity.

================================================================================
2. WHAT MAKES THIS DATA TRUSTWORTHY
================================================================================

The value of this data comes from a property most self-reported business
data lacks: it is CONFIRMED BY A COUNTERPARTY.

    - An order exists because a business placed it AND a supplier
      accepted it.
    - A delivery exists because a known party confirmed it at a known
      time.
    - A payment exists because one party marked it sent and the other
      confirmed receipt.

A business cannot unilaterally manufacture a trading history. That
bilateral confirmation is the foundation of everything in this document.

Signals that are NOT bilaterally confirmed (Loan Book entries, orders
against off-platform suppliers) are still captured, but are explicitly
weighted lower and flagged as self-reported. Conflating the two would
undermine the entire trust model.

================================================================================
3. DIGITAL BUSINESS PROFILE
================================================================================

    Business Since            registered_since
    Active Suppliers          distinct suppliers with >= 1 completed
                              order
    Orders Completed          count of completed orders
    Average Order Value       mean total_amount across completed orders
    Largest Order             maximum completed order value
    Repeat Supplier Rate      % of suppliers with more than one completed
                              order
    Order Frequency           orders per month, recency-weighted
    Delivery Confirmation     % of orders with a confirmed delivery
      Rate
    Payment Confirmation      % of delivered orders with a confirmed
      Rate                    payment
    Trading Consistency       regularity of activity over time
    Business Growth           change in order volume/value over a rolling
                              period

Displayed to the business as operational insight, not as a score to be
optimised.

================================================================================
4. SUPPLIER PERFORMANCE PROFILE
================================================================================

    Active Since              active_since
    Businesses Served         distinct businesses with completed orders
    Orders Completed          count
    Order Acceptance Rate     accepted / received
    Average Response Time     time from order placed to accepted/declined
    Fulfilment Time           accepted to dispatched
    Delivery Performance      dispatched to confirmed delivered
    Repeat Customer Rate      % of businesses ordering more than once

Response time and acceptance rate matter most: they are what a business
actually experiences as reliability.

================================================================================
5. RELATIONSHIP INTELLIGENCE
================================================================================

The business-supplier relationship, not the individual transaction, is
the fundamental unit of trust (TP-056).

Per relationship:
    - first_interaction_at, last_interaction_at
    - total_orders, total_order_value
    - order frequency and recency
    - completion rate within the pair
    - delivery and payment confirmation rates within the pair
    - relationship_health_score (composite)

Rationale: relationships are far harder to fabricate than transactions
and far more predictive of continued reliable behaviour. A business with
three deep, multi-year supplier relationships may present a stronger
trust profile than one with thirty one-off purchases.

================================================================================
6. BEHAVIOURAL SIGNALS
================================================================================

6.1 Order Behaviour
    Frequency, volume trend, value consistency, supplier concentration
    vs diversification, business-initiated cancellation rate.

6.2 Delivery Behaviour
    Confirmation rate, time-to-confirm, disputed or unconfirmed
    deliveries.

6.3 Payment Behaviour
    Time from delivery to payment confirmation, payment confirmation
    consistency, unpaid-after-delivery frequency.

    This is the single most financially predictive signal TRUConnect
    produces, precisely because it is bilaterally confirmed.

6.4 Credit Ledger Discipline  [self-reported -- weighted lower]
    Aggregate patterns only: settlement rate, average time to settle,
    proportion of book outstanding beyond 30/60/90 days.

    IMPORTANT: the Loan Book records the business's CUSTOMERS' debts to
    it. It is self-reported, unverified by any counterparty, and
    describes third parties who are not platform users.

    Rules:
        - Only AGGREGATE, DERIVED patterns may inform business signals.
        - Individual customer records must NEVER leave the business's own
          account, must never be shared with suppliers, and must never be
          exposed to any third party from within TRUConnect.
        - Because it is self-reported, credit ledger discipline is
          weighted substantially lower than bilaterally confirmed
          signals, and must be labelled as self-reported wherever it
          contributes to any derived output.

6.5 Platform Engagement
    Consistency of use, verification completion, profile completeness,
    offline sync integrity (high rejection or conflict rates may indicate
    data-quality problems rather than business problems).

================================================================================
7. TRUST SIGNALS vs RISK SIGNALS
================================================================================

Trust Signals (positive):
    Repeat orders within a relationship; sustained relationship duration;
    high delivery confirmation rate; consistent payment confirmation;
    verification completed; consistent trading over time.

Risk Signals (caution):
    High business-initiated cancellation rate; deliveries dispatched but
    never confirmed; recurring unpaid-after-delivery orders; sharply
    declining activity without explanation; lapsed verification;
    inconsistent or contradictory sync data.

Both are stored in the trust schema as discrete, queryable signals rather
than being baked into a single opaque number.

================================================================================
8. SCORING METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

8.1 Business Health Score
    Weighted composite of: order volume and frequency (recency-weighted),
    repeat supplier rate, delivery confirmation rate, payment
    confirmation rate, relationship health across suppliers, verification
    status, absence of risk signals.

8.2 Supplier Reputation Score
    Weighted composite of: orders completed, businesses served, repeat
    customer rate, acceptance rate, response time, delivery performance,
    verification status, longevity.

8.3 Methodology Rules
    - Weightings are CONFIGURABLE and VERSIONED, never hard-coded. Every
      stored score records the methodology_version that produced it.
    - Phase 1 scoring is transparent and rule-based, not machine
      learning. Explainability precedes automation (TP-029).
    - Scores must be explainable to the user in plain language: which
      behaviours raised or lowered them.
    - Self-reported inputs are weighted lower than bilaterally confirmed
      inputs, always.

================================================================================
9. RELATIONSHIP TO TRUSCORE  [IMPORTANT SCOPE BOUNDARY]
================================================================================

TRUConnect's scores are OPERATIONAL indicators. They exist to help
businesses and suppliers understand their own trading behaviour.

TRUScore is a separate, future, PLATFORM-LEVEL TruePoint capability
(Constitution Stage Six). It will consume TRUConnect's trust signals as
inputs, alongside signals from other future products.

Therefore, within TRUConnect:
    - Scores are NEVER described as credit scores.
    - Scores NEVER determine access to any financial product.
    - Scores are NEVER shared with lenders or third parties.
    - No lending eligibility, pre-qualification, or credit-readiness
      language appears anywhere in the product.

This boundary is constitutional (TP-084: Infrastructure Before Finance).
Earlier versions of this document included "Future Credit Readiness"
framing inside TRUConnect; that framing is removed here.

================================================================================
10. ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE (FUTURE, PLATFORM-LEVEL)
================================================================================

No AI is implemented in TRUConnect.

When platform intelligence eventually exists (TP-026 to TP-030), it will:
    - Interpret trusted data; never create it.
    - Be a shared TruePoint platform capability, not an isolated
      TRUConnect feature.
    - Remain explainable, supported by observable business evidence.
    - Never bypass the operational infrastructure beneath it.

Signals defined in this document are the eventual feature inputs. Nothing
in TRUConnect should be built in a way that depends on AI existing.

================================================================================
11. DATA OWNERSHIP AND RESPONSIBLE USE
================================================================================

    - Businesses own their operational data (TP-025). TruePoint is a
      steward, not an owner.
    - Supplier-facing views of a business respect Business Privacy: a
      supplier sees the trading relationship it participates in, never a
      business's wider trading profile, other supplier relationships, or
      Loan Book.
    - Loan Book data describes third parties (the business's customers)
      who are not platform users and have not consented to platform
      participation. It receives the strictest handling in the product
      and never leaves the owning business's account.
    - Data is used for operational, analytical, and platform-improvement
      purposes only, in compliance with applicable data protection law.
    - Trust cannot exist without responsible stewardship. A single
      mishandling of business data would damage the platform's core asset
      more than any feature could compensate for.
