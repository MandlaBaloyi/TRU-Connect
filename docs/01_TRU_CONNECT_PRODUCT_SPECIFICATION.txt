1. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

TRU Connect helps businesses buy better, helps suppliers sell more, and
quietly builds trusted business data in the background.

Users should never feel like they are completing credit applications or
creating financial records. Every normal business activity — searching,
asking questions, ordering, receiving deliveries — contributes to a trusted
digital business profile without requiring any deliberate data-entry effort
from the user.

Guiding product principles (elaborated in Section 10):

    - Simple First
    - Business Focused
    - Component Driven
    - Mobile First
    - Trust by Design
    - Scalable Architecture

================================================================================
2. APPLICATION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

TRU Connect is a single React Native mobile application serving two distinct
user experiences from one shared codebase, backend, and database.

                              TRU CONNECT
                                   |
                  -------------------------------------
                  |                                     |
          FOR BUSINESSES                        FOR SUPPLIERS
          (Primary Users)                       (Secondary Users)

Both experiences share the following backend systems:

    - Authentication
    - Database
    - Notifications
    - Product Catalogue
    - Orders
    - Messaging / Business Questions
    - Verification

Only the presented features and navigation differ according to the
authenticated user's role (business vs. supplier).

================================================================================
3. ONE APP, TWO EXPERIENCES
================================================================================

3.1 Role Determination
    A single account is associated with exactly one role at a time
    (business or supplier). Role is established at registration and
    determines which dashboard, navigation set, and feature set the user
    sees after authentication.

3.2 Shared Infrastructure, Divergent UI
    The application shell (authentication, notifications, settings, support)
    is shared. Role-specific modules (dashboards, catalogues, order
    management) are rendered conditionally based on role, using the shared
    component library defined in Document 03.

4. BUSINESS USER JOURNEY
================================================================================

    Launch App
        |
    Landing Screen (role selection: Business / Supplier)
        |
    Authentication (Login / Register / Continue with Google)
        |
    Business Dashboard (Home)
        |
    Daily Operations:
        - Browse / search suppliers
        - View supplier profiles and products
        - Ask Business Questions on products
        - Bookmark / follow suppliers
        - Place orders
        - Track order status and delivery
        - View and manage Digital Business Profile
        - Manage business documents and verification
        - Receive notifications (order updates, responses, promotions)

4.1 First-Time Business Experience
    1. View landing screen introduction slides (business-focused).
    2. Register or continue with Google.
    3. Complete lightweight business profile (name, category, location).
    4. Land on Home dashboard with guided prompts (e.g. "Find your first
       supplier").
    5. Begin building Digital Business Profile automatically as they browse
       and transact.

================================================================================
5. SUPPLIER USER JOURNEY
================================================================================

    Launch App
        |
    Landing Screen (role selection: Business / Supplier)
        |
    Authentication (Login / Register / Continue with Google)
        |
    Supplier Dashboard (Home)
        |
    Daily Operations:
        - Manage product catalogue
        - Receive and respond to orders
        - Respond to Business Questions on products
        - Publish Business Updates (promotions, new products, hours)
        - View customer enquiries
        - Track order fulfilment and delivery
        - View business performance and statistics
        - Manage verification and supplier documents

5.1 First-Time Supplier Experience
    1. View landing screen introduction slides (supplier-focused).
    2. Register or continue with Google.
    3. Complete supplier profile (business name, category, regions served).
    4. Upload initial product catalogue.
    5. Begin receiving business enquiries and orders; profile statistics
       accrue automatically.

6. NAVIGATION
================================================================================

6.1 Business Bottom Navigation (5 tabs)
    - Home       — daily overview and recommendations
    - Business    — profile, verification, wallet, insights, documents
    - Suppliers   — discovery, search, categories, saved suppliers
    - Orders      — current, pending, completed, cancelled orders
    - More        — settings, support, account

6.2 Supplier Bottom Navigation (5 tabs)
    - Home        — daily overview and performance
    - Products    — catalogue management
    - Orders      — order fulfilment and history
    - Customers   — enquiries and customer statistics
    - More        — settings, support, account

Navigation structure is defined in detail, including component composition,
in Document 03 (Frontend Component Specification).

7.3 Supplier Dashboard Module
    - Welcome / supplier summary card

7.4 Supplier Discovery Module (Business-facing)
    - Search
    - Categories
    - Nearby Suppliers
    - Featured Suppliers

    - Recently Viewed
    - Saved Suppliers

7.5 Product Catalogue Module (Supplier-facing)
    - Product listing management
    - Categories

    - Availability and stock status
    - Minimum order quantities

7.6 Orders Module (Shared)
    - Order placement (business)
    - Order acceptance / rejection (supplier)
    - Order status tracking
    - Invoices
    - Delivery tracking
    - Order history (current, pending, completed, cancelled)

7.7 Business Questions Module (Shared)
    - Ask a question beneath a product (business)
    - Respond to a question (supplier)
    - Questions and answers remain attached to the product for future
      visibility (see Document 09 for rationale).

7.8 Verification Module
    - Business verification
    - Supplier verification
    - Verification badge display across profiles and cards

7.9 Notifications Module
    - Order status changes
    - New responses to Business Questions
    - Business Updates from followed/saved suppliers
    - System and account notifications

7.10 Digital Business Profile Module
    - Automatically generated business statistics (orders, suppliers,
      average order value, repeat supplier rate, payment behaviour,
      business growth, risk level)
    - See Document 07 for the underlying trust engine.

7.11 Business Updates Module (Supplier-facing)
    - Publish updates: new products, promotions, price changes, holiday
      hours, new locations, service announcements.

9. USER ROLES
================================================================================

9.1 Business (Primary)
    Permissions: search/browse suppliers, view public supplier profiles,
    ask Business Questions, bookmark suppliers, place orders, manage own
    business profile and documents, view own Digital Business Profile.

9.2 Supplier (Secondary)
    Permissions: manage own product catalogue, respond to Business
    Questions, accept/reject/fulfil orders, publish Business Updates,
    view own customer statistics (aggregated, privacy-preserving — see
    Document 09, Business Privacy).

9.3 Future Roles (Not in Phase 1 scope, noted for architecture readiness)
    - Platform Administrator (internal moderation, verification review)
    - Financial Partner (read-only access to aggregated trust data via
      future partner APIs — Phase 4)

10. PRODUCT DESIGN PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

Every screen and feature within TRU Connect must follow these principles:

    1. Simple First — Prioritise clarity over visual complexity. Every
       screen should answer one clear question for the user.
    2. Business Focused — Every feature should help businesses buy, sell,
       or grow. Features that do not serve this goal are out of scope.
    3. Component Driven — Build reusable components (Document 03) that
       scale consistently across both business and supplier experiences.
    4. Mobile First — Design for fast, intuitive use on smartphones,
       including on lower-end devices and inconsistent connectivity.
    5. Trust by Design — Verification, transparency, and security are
       embedded throughout the experience, not bolted on afterward.
    6. Scalable Architecture — Every feature must support future expansion
       into supplier services, financial products, and institutional
       partnerships without requiring architectural rework.
