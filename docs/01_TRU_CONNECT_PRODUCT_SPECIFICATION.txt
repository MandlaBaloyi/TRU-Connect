1. PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

TRUConnect digitizes the supplier relationships an informal business
already has, and the customer credit it already extends.

It replaces:
    WhatsApp order threads. Phone calls. SMS. Paper order books.
    Handwritten credit ledgers. Memory.

It does not replace:
    The relationships themselves.

Every feature must answer one question: "What business problem does this
solve today?" (TP-016, TP-049). Features that only make sense once
lending, AI, or payments exist do not belong in TRUConnect.

Governing product principles (elaborated in Section 10):
    Simple First - Operational First - Mobile First - Offline Aware -
    Component Driven - Trust By Design - Scalable Architecture

================================================================================
2. APPLICATION OVERVIEW
================================================================================

TRUConnect is a single React Native mobile application serving two
distinct user experiences from one shared backend, database, and identity
platform (Constitution TP-032: One User Identity).

                              TRUCONNECT
                                   |
                  -------------------------------------
                  |                                     |
          INFORMAL BUSINESSES                     SUPPLIERS
          (Primary Users)                         (Secondary Users)
          Business Free                           Free -> Pro -> Enterprise

Shared platform services:
    - Authentication & Authorization (single users table, RBAC)
    - Notifications (shared notification infrastructure)
    - Orders & Trade Events
    - Verification
    - Audit & Observability

Only the presented features and navigation differ by role. Supplier Pro
additionally gains a web dashboard (frontend/web); Business remains
mobile-only.

================================================================================
3. USER TIERS
================================================================================

3.1 Business Free (Primary)
    Free, permanently. The platform cannot build trusted infrastructure
    without active businesses (TP-046).

    Capabilities: Business Profile, My Suppliers, Order Queue, Orders,
    Loan Book, Customers, Notifications, Offline Support.

3.2 Supplier Free
    Free. Exists so that a business's existing suppliers can participate
    without a purchase decision.

    Capabilities: Receive Orders, Update Order Status, Provide Payment
    Reference, Confirm Delivery, basic business profile.

3.3 Supplier Pro
    Paid. For suppliers who have outgrown order-taking alone.

    Adds: Product Management (catalogue), Staff Users, Reporting, Web
    Dashboard.

3.4 Supplier Enterprise
    Paid. For suppliers operating at scale.

    Adds: Teams and role hierarchies, Advanced Reporting, ERP
    Integration, API access.

Tier capability is enforced through the existing RBAC role/permission
system -- not through duplicate user implementations (TP-032).

================================================================================
4. BUSINESS USER JOURNEY
================================================================================

    Launch App
        |
    Landing / Onboarding
        |
    Authentication (Register / Sign In / Continue with Google)
        |
    Verify (OTP)
        |
    Business Profile Setup
        |
    ADD MY SUPPLIERS  <-- the defining first action
        |
    Daily Operations

4.1 The Defining First Action: Add My Suppliers
    A new business does not search a catalogue. It records the suppliers
    it ALREADY buys from -- by name and contact number, in seconds.

    - If the supplier is already on TRUConnect, the relationship links
      immediately and orders flow digitally.
    - If the supplier is not yet on TRUConnect, the business can still
      record orders against that supplier for its own records, and may
      invite the supplier to join.

    This is the single most important design decision in the product. The
    business gets value from day one, with or without supplier adoption
    (TP-019).

4.2 Daily Operations (Business)
    - Place an order with an existing supplier (repeat-order in a few
      taps, using previous orders as the template).
    - Track order status: placed, accepted, dispatched, delivered.
    - Confirm delivery and record payment reference.
    - Record a credit sale in the Loan Book, and mark it settled when the
      customer pays.
    - View trading history and business profile.
    - Work offline; actions queue and synchronise automatically.

================================================================================
5. SUPPLIER USER JOURNEY
================================================================================

    Launch App (or accept a business's invitation)
        |
    Authentication -> Verify -> Supplier Profile Setup
        |
    Daily Operations

5.1 Daily Operations (Supplier Free)
    - See every incoming order in ONE queue.
    - Accept or decline an order, with a reason.
    - Update order status through to delivery.
    - Provide a payment reference for the business to pay against
      directly.
    - Confirm delivery.

5.2 Additional Operations (Supplier Pro)
    - Manage a product catalogue so businesses order against real items
      and prices instead of free-text.
    - Add staff users who can process orders.
    - View reporting on orders, customers, and fulfilment.
    - Operate from a web dashboard rather than mobile only.

5.3 Additional Operations (Supplier Enterprise)
    - Teams with role hierarchies across branches or regions.
    - Advanced reporting and export.
    - ERP integration and API access.

6. NAVIGATION
================================================================================

The implemented mobile application uses a five-tab bottom navigation per
role. This specification aligns the CONTENT of those tabs with the
Constitution; the navigation structure itself is unchanged, preserving
compatibility with the existing codebase.

6.1 Business Navigation
    Home        Today's operational picture: pending orders, orders
                awaiting delivery, outstanding Loan Book balance, recent
                activity.
    Account     Operational workspace: My Suppliers, Order Queue, Loan
                Book, Customers, Business Profile, Verification,
                Documents, Settings.
    Community   Business-to-business knowledge and platform updates.
                NOT a marketplace and NOT supplier promotion. Optional,
                and lower priority than operational features.
    Discovery   Ecosystem content: platform updates, verified supplier
                announcements, programmes, campaigns. Late-phase supplier
                discovery lives here (Section 7.9), deliberately
                secondary.
    More        Settings, support, help, legal, logout.

6.2 Supplier Navigation
    Home        Incoming orders, orders to dispatch, deliveries pending,
                today's activity.
    Account     Operational workspace: Orders, Deliveries, Customers,
                Products (Pro), Staff (Pro), Reporting (Pro), Profile,
                Settings.
    Community   Supplier announcements and platform updates.
    Discovery   Ecosystem content and platform announcements.
    More        Settings, support, help, legal, logout.

7. FEATURES & MODULES
================================================================================

7.1 Authentication (IMPLEMENTED)
    Register (business or supplier), Sign In, Continue with Google, OTP
    verification, Forgot/Reset Password, JWT with refresh-token rotation,
    logout with token revocation, RBAC.

    Registration is deliberately minimal: only what is needed to create
    an account. Business detail is collected later, in Profile Setup.

7.2 Business Profile
    Business name, trading name, category, location, contact, trading
    information, documents. Feeds verification and, much later, TRUScore.

7.3 My Suppliers  [replaces the former "Supplier Discovery" module]
    Purpose: manage the suppliers this business ALREADY trades with.

    Problem solved: today a business keeps supplier numbers in its phone
    contacts and its order history in WhatsApp. Nothing connects them.

    Components:
        - Add a supplier (name + phone; seconds, minimal typing)
        - My Suppliers list (most recently ordered first)
        - Supplier detail: contact, order history with this supplier,
          outstanding orders, typical items
        - Invite supplier to TRUConnect
        - Saved/preferred supplier ordering

7.4 Order Queue
    Purpose: a single place showing every order that still needs
    something to happen.

    Problem solved: orders currently live scattered across WhatsApp
    threads, with no reliable "what is outstanding right now" view.

    Business view: awaiting acceptance, accepted, dispatched, awaiting
    delivery confirmation, awaiting payment.
    Supplier view: new, accepted, being prepared, dispatched, delivered.

7.5 Orders
    Place order, repeat previous order, accept/decline (supplier),
    status progression, delivery confirmation, payment reference and
    payment confirmation, order history, order detail.

    Repeat-order is a first-class action: most informal business ordering
    is repetitive, and re-entering it every time is pure friction
    (TP-059, TP-061).

7.6 Loan Book
    Purpose: record which CUSTOMERS owe this business money from credit
    sales, and track settlement.

    Problem solved: credit sales are recorded in a paper book that is
    lost, damaged, miscounted, or disputed. This is one of the most
    universally reported operational pains for spaza shops and similar
    businesses.

    Components:
        - Record a credit sale (customer, amount, date, optional note)
        - Outstanding balance per customer and in total
        - Record a repayment (full or partial)
        - Settlement history
        - Reminders

    IMPORTANT: The Loan Book is a RECORD-KEEPING tool. TRUConnect does
    not lend, does not hold money, and does not collect on the business's
    behalf. It replaces the paper book, nothing more.

7.7 Customers
    Purpose: a light record of the business's repeat customers, primarily
    to support the Loan Book.

    Components: customer list, contact, credit history, outstanding
    balance. Deliberately minimal -- this is not a CRM.

7.8 Product Catalogue (Supplier Pro)
    Purpose: let businesses order against real items and prices.

    Problem solved: free-text ordering causes errors, price disputes, and
    repeated clarifying phone calls.

    Components: product list, create/edit product, pricing, availability,
    minimum order quantity, product images, drafts.

7.9 Supplier Discovery  [LATE PHASE, DELIBERATELY SECONDARY]
    Purpose: help a business find a genuinely NEW supplier on the rare
    occasions it needs one -- a new product line, a supplier that closed,
    relocation.

    This is NOT the core loop and must never be presented as the primary
    way to use TRUConnect. It lives inside Discovery, arrives in a later
    phase (Document 08), and exists only because the need occasionally
    exists in reality.

7.10 Notifications
    Order placed, accepted, declined, dispatched, delivered; payment
    confirmed; supplier joined by invitation; verification status; Loan
    Book reminders; platform announcements.

7.11 Verification
    Business and supplier verification with a verification badge. Feeds
    trust signals (Document 07).

7.12 Offline Support
    Read access to cached suppliers, orders, and Loan Book entries.
    Write actions queue locally and synchronise automatically when
    connectivity returns (TP-062). The user should rarely have to think
    about connectivity.

9. USER ROLES & PERMISSIONS
================================================================================

Implemented today (single users table + RBAC, TP-032):
    business    Business Free user.
    supplier    Supplier user; tier determines capability.
    admin       Reserved platform administrator role.

Planned, additive (no new user tables):
    supplier_staff        Staff member acting for a Supplier Pro account.
    supplier_enterprise   Enterprise supplier with team management.
    lender                Future, read-only, out of TRUConnect scope.

Supplier tier is an attribute of the supplier account resolved into
permissions -- not a separate identity model.

================================================================================
10. PRODUCT DESIGN PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

    1. Simple First -- fewer taps, less reading, less typing, less
       training (TP-061).
    2. Operational First -- every feature solves a problem the business
       has TODAY (TP-049).
    3. Mobile First -- one-handed operation, large touch targets, fast
       startup, low-end device tolerance (TP-018, TP-060).
    4. Offline Aware -- graceful behaviour on intermittent connectivity
       (TP-062).
    5. Component Driven -- reusable components across both experiences.
    6. Trust By Design -- verification and transparency embedded, never
       bolted on.
    7. Scalable Architecture -- every feature must support millions of
       businesses without rework (TP-038).

================================================================================
11. FUTURE EXPANSION
================================================================================

Within TRUConnect (Document 08): Supplier Pro capabilities, Supplier
Enterprise capabilities, richer business insights, late-phase supplier
discovery.

Beyond TRUConnect (TruePoint platform stages, separate products):
TRUScore, TRUWallet, TRUPayLater.

TRUConnect itself will NOT become a wallet, a payment processor, or a
lender. It produces the trusted operational record those later products
depend on (TP-084).
