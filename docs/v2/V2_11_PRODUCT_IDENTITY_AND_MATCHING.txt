===========================================================================
V2_11 — PRODUCT IDENTITY AND MATCHING
       Identity · Evidence · Decision · Governance
===========================================================================

Written 2026-09-11, from an architecture proposal made during review.
Supersedes nothing; extends the matching ladder built in V2 step 6 and
the verification curtain in V2_03 Part 4.

THE ORGANISING PRINCIPLE, and it is the right one:

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DO NOT MAKE AI THE SOURCE OF TRUTH FOR PRODUCT IDENTITY.
  GTIN and deterministic rules are the source of truth.
  AI is the RECOGNITION layer for when supplier data is messy.
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Everything below follows from that sentence.


===========================================================================
PART 1 — WHAT A GTIN ACTUALLY GUARANTEES
===========================================================================

Confirmed against GS1's own definition, because the whole design rests
on it:

  ONE GTIN identifies ONE TRADE ITEM, and may be printed on unlimited
  physical units of that same item. Every Coca-Cola Original 2L PET
  bottle on earth carries 5449000054227.

  DIFFERENT VARIATIONS REQUIRE DIFFERENT GTINs -- size, flavour,
  variety, packaging. Coke 330ml, Coke 500ml, Coke 2L, Coke Zero 2L and
  a 6-pack of Coke 2L are five trade items and five GTINs.

So a GTIN is the strongest identity signal available, and it is exactly
what a shared catalogue needs.

1.1 THREE THINGS IT DOES NOT GUARANTEE

  NOT EVERY PRODUCT HAS ONE. Fresh produce, informal and home-made
  goods, small manufacturers without GS1 membership, supplier-created
  bundles, services. In this market that is not an edge case, it is a
  large minority. GTIN is the strongest signal, never the only one.

  ONE PRODUCT CAN HAVE SEVERAL. A bottle carries its GTIN-13; the case
  of twelve carries a different GTIN-14. A supplier selling by the case
  will give us the CASE code, which will not match the bottle's row.

  This is a known and accepted cost, already recorded in product.py:
  "case barcodes (GTIN-14) no longer map one-to-one onto products. A
  scanned case code resolves through the OFFER; a consumer code
  resolves through this table." The catalogue product is deliberately
  the CONSUMER UNIT so that a supplier selling 6-packs still compares
  against one selling 12-packs.

  OPEN DECISION: accept the miss and let case codes fall through to
  text matching, or add a gtin_aliases table mapping additional codes
  to a product with a pack multiplier. The second is more correct and
  more work. Not MVP either way.

  THE CODE CAN BE ON THE WRONG PRODUCT. A supplier typing the
  cooldrink's barcode onto a juice produces a VALID barcode attached to
  the wrong thing. The check digit cannot help -- the number is sound.
  Part 3 is the answer.


===========================================================================
PART 2 — THE FOUR LAYERS
===========================================================================

  IDENTITY     GTIN and other strong identifiers. Deterministic.
  EVIDENCE     name, brand, size, pack, category, image, OCR.
  DECISION     rules + similarity + AI confidence + supplier trust.
  GOVERNANCE   AUTO_LINK / REVIEW / CREATE_NEW / REJECT, and the audit.

The value of separating them is that each can change without the others.
Swapping the AI provider touches EVIDENCE only. Tightening who may
auto-link touches GOVERNANCE only.


===========================================================================
PART 3 — CONFIDENCE IS MULTI-DIMENSIONAL, AND THIS FIXES THE JUICE BUG
===========================================================================

Today tier 0 returns 1.0 on a barcode hit and links. That is what lets a
mistyped or mis-entered barcode produce a confident wrong link.

The fix is not to distrust barcodes. It is to notice when signals
CONTRADICT each other:

    GTIN match        1.00        GTIN match        1.00
    brand             0.99        brand             0.05
    name              0.96        name              0.02
    size              1.00        size              0.10
    category          1.00        category          0.02
    ───────────────────────       ───────────────────────
    -> AUTO_LINK                  -> REVIEW, not a link

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  A barcode match whose name, brand and size all disagree is evidence
  of an ERROR, not evidence of a match.
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

This is the same reasoning already in score_rules_match, where a pack
disagreement costs -0.40 precisely because "Coke 2L" and "Coke 500ml"
read alike and are not the same product. Extended to tier 0.

CHEAP TO IMPLEMENT: the similarity computation already exists and runs
in microseconds. Tier 0 gains a consistency check, not a new subsystem.


===========================================================================
PART 4 — THREE CORRECTIONS TO THE PROPOSED PIPELINE
===========================================================================

4.1 READING A BARCODE FROM AN IMAGE IS FREE. IT IS NOT AI.

  The proposal grouped "OCR / barcode detection / image understanding"
  together under a rented model. They are different things with very
  different costs.

    DECODING a barcode from a photograph is a solved, deterministic,
    LOCAL problem -- pyzbar, ZXing, OpenCV. No network, no per-call
    cost, no model. If the barcode is visible in the picture, we can
    read it for nothing.

    EXTRACTING ATTRIBUTES from a picture ("this looks like Coca-Cola
    Original, 2L, PET") needs a vision model and costs money per call.

  THE HIGHEST-VALUE IMAGE OPERATION IS THE FREE ONE, and it should be
  tried first on every uploaded image:

      image -> local barcode decoder -> GTIN -> tier 0
            -> nothing found -> everything else

  A supplier who photographs the front of a bottle gives us nothing; one
  who photographs the back gives us an exact identity for zero rand.
  That is worth saying in the upload UI.

4.2 SKU IS NOT A MATCHING TIER

  supplier_id + SKU already exists, and the proposal is right that it is
  never globally unique. But it is not a tier in the catalogue-matching
  ladder either, because a supplier's SKU says nothing about WHICH
  catalogue product something is. It identifies THE OFFER, and it is
  already the upsert key on re-import.

  THERE IS A BETTER VERSION OF WHAT THIS WAS REACHING FOR, and it is a
  large cost saving:

      If an offer ALREADY has a product_id, and its identity fields
      (name, barcode, brand, pack) are unchanged since last import,
      SKIP MATCHING ENTIRELY.

  A supplier sends a price file every week and only the prices move.
  Today every row is re-matched -- roughly five database round trips
  each, measured. Skipping unchanged rows removes that for what will
  typically be 90-95% of a recurring file. Deterministic, no model, and
  it compounds with the batching work already done.

4.3 IMAGE SIMILARITY CANNOT BE A CONFIDENCE DIMENSION YET

  The proposal lists image_score alongside name and brand. It cannot
  work in that direction at first, because there is nothing to compare
  against: catalogue.products.image_url is nullable and, in its own
  words, "expected to be null for most rows for a long time".

  So image evidence flows ONE WAY to begin with:

      supplier image -> attributes -> matched as TEXT against the
      catalogue

  Image-to-image similarity becomes possible only once the catalogue
  has images, which is itself a later decision (and V2_04 Part 4 chose
  CATEGORY art rather than product art, deliberately).


===========================================================================
PART 5 — TWO THINGS THE PROPOSAL DID NOT COVER
===========================================================================

5.1 WHO OWNS THE CANONICAL NAME

  The first supplier to create a catalogue product names it. Without a
  rule, "COKE ORIGINAL 2LT PET BOTTLE" becomes the canonical name of
  that product forever, and every business browsing sees it.

  THIS CHANGES WHAT THE REVIEWER DOES. The CREATE_NEW review is not
  only "is this allowed to be sold" (V2_03 Part 4.3) -- it is also "is
  this named properly". A reviewer approving a new catalogue product is
  performing an editorial act, and the screen should let them correct
  the name, brand and pack rather than only accept or reject.

  The supplier's own wording is never lost: it stays on their offer,
  which is what their own screens show them.

5.2 VISION HAS TO HAVE A CEILING

  A supplier with 3,000 products and an image each is 3,000 vision
  calls. That is real money and it arrives in one burst at import time.

  RULES:
    · vision runs only after the free tiers have failed -- the tail,
      not the default
    · vision runs only when a local barcode decode found nothing
    · it goes through the SAME spend guard as the text AI tier
      (shared/ai/decisions/spend_guard), and it is off by default
    · it may be QUEUED rather than inline. An offer can exist unmatched
      for an hour without harming anyone; blocking a supplier's import
      on 3,000 model calls definitely does.


===========================================================================
PART 6 — TRUST AND CONFIDENCE ARE DIFFERENT AXES
===========================================================================

The sharpest idea in the proposal, and it is correct:

    A verified supplier can still submit a bad match.
    An AI can be confident about a product from an untrustworthy source.

So they are never combined into one number. The policy engine takes
both, plus the evidence, and decides.

  SUPPLIER TRUST
    UNVERIFIED   may create, upload, draft, prepare. May NOT link to
                 the canonical catalogue and may NOT create canonical
                 products. Their offers are invisible to businesses
                 (V2_03 Part 4) and invisible to the catalogue.
    VERIFIED     may match and may request new canonical products.
                 High-risk matches still go to review.
    TRUSTED      earned. More automation permitted.

  WHY UNVERIFIED SUPPLIERS MUST NOT TOUCH THE CATALOGUE, stated
  plainly: anyone can register and upload test data. If that reaches
  the canonical catalogue, the shared library every business relies on
  is polluted by people who were only trying the app out. The catalogue
  is the moat (V2_02); an unguarded write path into it is the fastest
  way to destroy it.

6.1 TRUST MUST BE MEASURED, OR IT IS SELF-CONFIRMING

  "98% correct matches, therefore TRUSTED, therefore auto-link" only
  works if correctness is still being checked. If trust removes review
  entirely, nothing measures accuracy any more and the number stays at
  98% forever by construction.

  SO: even a TRUSTED supplier has a small random sample reviewed --
  enough to notice a drift, not enough to be a burden. Trust is a
  reduced review rate, never a zero review rate.

6.2 THE 1-3 DAY VERIFICATION WINDOW

  The instinct to state a window is good, and the reasoning behind it
  should be precise:

    THE DELAY IS NOT THE SECURITY MECHANISM. PENDING is. An unverified
    supplier is harmless not because three days have passed but because
    their offers are invisible and cannot reach the catalogue.

    The 1-3 days is a CONSEQUENCE of human review capacity, not a
    control. It follows that we should not advertise a window we cannot
    staff -- and with one reviewer (V2_09 Part 6), "1-3 business days"
    is a promise that breaks the first time ten suppliers register on a
    Friday.

    RECOMMENDATION: tell the supplier what is happening and what they
    can do meanwhile ("your shop is ready, nobody can see it yet"),
    and quote a time only once it is measured.


===========================================================================
PART 7 — THE EVIDENCE RECORD
===========================================================================

MatchReview already stores source_text, proposed_product_id, tier,
confidence, reason, status, decided_by, decided_at. It answers "what
happened". It does not answer "why", because confidence is a single
number.

WHAT TO ADD: the per-dimension scores, the model and version if one was
involved, and the decision path.

    gtin_score  name_score  brand_score  size_score  category_score
    image_score  overall_score
    ai_provider  ai_model_version
    policy_version

  WHY IT IS WORTH THE COLUMNS:

    DEBUGGING. "Why is this supplier's juice linked to Coca-Cola" has a
    real answer instead of a shrug.

    DISPUTES. A supplier challenging a match is owed the reasoning, and
    V2_00 already commits to that: "so a disputed match can be
    explained".

    AND IT IS THE TRAINING SET. Every reviewed decision, with its
    features and its human verdict, is a labelled example on a
    genuinely hard case. Recording the features at decision time is the
    difference between having a dataset in a year and wishing you had
    started one.

  Store WHO decided -- human or model, and which model. A later audit
  must be able to separate "a person said so" from "a model said so".


===========================================================================
PART 8 — THE ORDER TO BUILD IT
===========================================================================

Deliberately not "build the pipeline". Each step is useful alone and
none of them requires the next.

  NOW, AND ALL FREE
   1  Tier 0 consistency check (Part 3). Fixes the juice bug.
   2  Skip matching on unchanged re-imported rows (4.2). Large saving.
   3  Local barcode decode from uploaded images (4.1). Free identity.
   4  Evidence columns on MatchReview (Part 7). Start the dataset.
   5  Unverified suppliers cannot reach the catalogue (Part 6).
      This is V2_07 item K and is already MVP.

  NEXT, AND STILL FREE
   6  The policy engine as one place, rather than rules scattered
      through services.
   7  Canonical-name editing in the CREATE_NEW review (5.1).

  THEN, AND IT COSTS
   8  Vision attribute extraction for the tail, behind the spend guard,
      queued rather than inline (5.2).

  LATER, ONCE THERE ARE LABELS
   9  Learned ranking over the recorded features.
  10  A TruConnect matching model, if and only if it beats the rules at
      zero wrong links.

  THE BAR REMAINS WHAT IT WAS. Hand-written rules took free-tier
  resolution from 40% to 80% with zero wrong links. Anything added here
  has to beat that, on the same measure, or it is complexity for its
  own sake.


===========================================================================
PART 9 — THE RULE THIS DOCUMENT EXISTS TO ESTABLISH
===========================================================================

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  A SUPPLIER NEVER DEFINES CANONICAL PRODUCT IDENTITY MERELY BY
  SUBMITTING A PRODUCT.

      submission -> evidence -> matching -> policy -> catalogue

  and for something genuinely new:

      submission -> CREATE_NEW candidate -> review -> catalogue
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The catalogue does not belong to the supplier who happened to upload
first. It belongs to TruConnect, and every business that will one day
compare prices across it depends on that being true.
