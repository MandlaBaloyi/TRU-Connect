===========================================================================
V2_18 — THE FIRST FIVE MINUTES: FUEL, AND THE COLUMNS THAT NEVER BURNED
===========================================================================

Written 2026-09-15. A PLAN, and an audit.

WHAT THIS DOCUMENT IS. V2_15 built a demand engine and V2_17 taught the
feed to survive scale. Both assumed a shop that had told us something.
Nothing in the product ever asked.

Mandla named the gap in one sentence:

    "these r not jsu questions these r the data they feed to the emgine
     / this is the fuel we give to our community engine"

And named the failure it causes:

    "coz there community tab, must tell an informal tht wait here i can
     find suppliers, but sock orders, seacrh my own suppiliers, invite
     mur own suppliers, than going on community tab and its blank"

So this document does three things. It audits every column on
business_profiles against what actually reads it, decides which of them
earn a place in a mandatory signup flow, and lays out the build order
for that flow plus the four smaller repairs the audit turned up on the
way.


===========================================================================
PART 1 — THE AUDIT
===========================================================================

Method: every column on BusinessProfile, grepped against the whole of
backend/src/, with the profile domain's own read/write excluded. A
column that only its own service touches is a column that only its own
screen touches.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1.1  FUEL — code outside the profile domain reads it
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

      buys_categories       declared.py:67. Every category-driven rail,
                            and the gate on the sponsored slot.
      restock_rhythm        needs_service.py:123 -> cadence_days. The
                            timing of everything.
      weekly_spend_band     needs_service.py:128 -> order_ceiling_cents.
                            Basket sizing.
      customer_types        weighting.py:147 -> prefers_bulk.
      latitude/longitude    feed_detail_service.py:201, collect near
                            you, not ordered yet, businesses near you.
      business_name         Identity, everywhere. Already NOT NULL.

  These six are the fuel. Nothing else on the table is.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1.2  UTILITY — the app needs it, the engine does not
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

      city_or_town, province, phone, email, whatsapp and their flags,
      normalized_phone, normalized_email, business_image_url,
      visible_image_source, photo_visibility, category, description.

  Find Business, contact, avatars, profile previews. Real work, none of
  it the engine's.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1.3  DEAD — written by the profile screen, read by nothing
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

      business_type         SOLE_TRADER / PARTNERSHIP / ...
      operating_model       HOME / SHOP / MARKET_STALL / ...
      years_operating
      supplier_sources      an entire section, "Suppliers"
      operating_days
      opening_time
      closing_time
      employee_range        carried into DeclaredSignal and then never
                            used by needs_service
      physical_address
      business_info_complete
      location_complete

  Eleven columns and two whole sections of a screen that produce no
  effect anywhere in the product.


===========================================================================
PART 2 — WHAT TO DO WITH THE DEAD LIST
===========================================================================

Mandla asked which columns we are dropping. The honest answer is that
"drop" is the wrong verb for nine of the eleven.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  THE RULE
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

      A FIELD EARNS A PLACE IN THE MANDATORY STEPS ONLY IF A NAMED
      PIECE OF CODE READS IT.

      Everything else is demoted from the ASK, not from the TABLE.

  WHY NOT DROP THEM. A nullable column nobody queries costs nothing:
  no join, no index, no migration risk, no confusion at the call site
  because there is no call site. A QUESTION at signup costs something
  real and measurable — a person who closes the app on step two. The
  asymmetry is the whole argument. Dropping is also irreversible, and
  at least two of these (operating_model, years_operating) are
  plausible fuel for work that has not been designed yet.

  So business_type, operating_model, years_operating, supplier_sources,
  operating_days, opening_time, closing_time, employee_range and
  physical_address stay on the table, stay editable in My Business for
  anyone who wants to fill them, and appear in NO onboarding step.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  THE TWO EXCEPTIONS, AND THEY GO ENTIRELY
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  business_info_complete and location_complete are not merely unused.
  Nothing has ever written them, so they are false on every row that
  has ever existed — and completionCalculator.ts already answers the
  same question client-side, correctly, from the data itself. Two
  columns holding a worse answer to a question already answered
  elsewhere are a trap for the next person who trusts them.

  They are also the backing for the VERIFICATION TAB, which is the
  larger problem:

      emailVerified is HARD-CODED False at
      business_profile_service.py:222.

  So the tab shows a checklist of four boxes, two of which can never
  tick, implying a review of an informal business that does not happen
  and that Mandla has decided should not. identity_verified and
  documents_verified were already removed from this model for exactly
  that reason; this is the rest of the same removal.

  REMOVED: the VERIFICATION tab, VerificationChecklist.tsx, the
  VerificationStatus type, the "verification" block in the profile
  payload, and both columns. The completion bar stays — it is the
  honest version of the same idea, and it measures how much this shop
  has told us rather than passing judgement on them.


===========================================================================
PART 3 — A BUG THE AUDIT TURNED UP
===========================================================================

A business's business_name is seeded from the OWNER'S PERSONAL NAME:

    otp_service.py:175
    default_name = " ".join([user.first_name, user.last_name]) or
                   "New business"

and businessName on BusinessRegisterSchema is accepted at registration
and never persisted. The column's own default is the empty string.

This is the identical gap already fixed on the supplier side. The
consequence is visible today: a supplier using Find Business sees their
customer listed as "Mandla Baloyi" rather than as a shop.

It is fixed here rather than in the setup flow because it is wrong
independently of whether the setup flow exists.


===========================================================================
PART 4 — THE MANDATORY STEPS
===========================================================================

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4.1  WHERE THE GATE LIVES
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  app/(dashboard)/index.tsx, which is ALREADY the single role fork —
  every authenticated path in the app passes through it, and it is the
  only file that knows the role before a dashboard is chosen. Business
  role plus incomplete setup redirects to /(setup)/step-1 instead of
  /business/home.

  Putting the gate anywhere else means putting it in several places.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4.2  THE THREE STEPS
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

      1  YOUR SHOP       business name, what stock you buy, who you
                         sell to
                         -> business_name, buys_categories,
                            customer_types
                         REQUIRED

      2  HOW YOU BUY     how often you restock, roughly what you spend
                         in a week
                         -> restock_rhythm, weekly_spend_band
                         REQUIRED

      3  WHERE YOU ARE   a pin on a map
                         -> latitude, longitude, city_or_town, province
                         SKIPPABLE, ONCE

  Six fields. Exactly the six in 1.1, in the order a person can answer
  them.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4.3  THREE RULES THAT KEEP IT HONEST
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  EVERY STEP SAYS WHAT IT CHANGES. Step one's footer reads "This is
  what fills your Community tab." This is not a new rule — it is the
  rule tell_us_what_you_buy already follows, quoted in its own
  docstring: only ask for what will visibly change the next screen, and
  say exactly what it will change. A signup flow that asks the same
  questions without the same promise is a worse version of a screen we
  already got right.

  STEP THREE GETS ONE SKIP. It needs a location permission or a map
  drag, and blocking a brand-new account behind a permission dialog is
  the single most expensive place in an app to lose somebody. Skipping
  costs exactly two rails — collect near you, not ordered from yet —
  and the feed can say so, which is a better conversation to have on
  day three than on minute one.

      DELEGATED BY MANDLA, decided here. Falsified by: a completion
      rate on step 3 far below steps 1 and 2 with no later recovery,
      which would mean the skip is a trapdoor rather than a deferral.

  A STEP ANSWERED HERE IS THE SAME EDIT AS IN MY BUSINESS. Same
  service, same validation, no second write path. My Business stays
  exactly where it is in the More tab, because a person who wants to
  change an answer later must be able to.

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  4.4  EXISTING ACCOUNTS ARE NOT PUSHED THROUGH IT
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  New accounts only. Thirty seeded shops and Mandla's own half-filled
  profile keep working untouched.

  WHY. A gate that appears in front of somebody who has already been
  using the app reads as a fault, not as a welcome — and the mechanism
  for catching those accounts already exists and is gentler: the
  tell_us_what_you_buy rail sits third in the feed, vanishes the moment
  it is answered, and asks inside the screen the answer improves.

      DELEGATED BY MANDLA, decided here. Falsified by: a long tail of
      pre-setup accounts whose feeds stay empty because the rail is
      being scrolled past rather than answered.

  Once setup exists, that rail correctly disappears for everybody who
  came through it — it is gated on "has declared nothing", which a
  completed step 1 makes false.

PART 5 — WHAT ELSE IS STILL MISSING IN "FOR YOU"
===========================================================================

Recorded here because the work was halted mid-flight and the list
otherwise lives only in conversation. Worst first.

  1  THE CATALOGUE IS STILL NOT A CATALOGUE. 271 product names are
     stocked by more than one supplier; 0 offers are matched to a
     canonical product. So alternativeCount is 0 on every card,
     "compare suppliers" cannot exist, and the price-movement rail
     V2_09 defers can never be built — a price only moves if two offers
     are known to be the same thing. V2_11 defines the matching;
     nothing runs it.

  2  NOTHING RECORDS THAT A CARD WAS SEEN. Declared answers and
     confirmed orders only. No impressions, no taps. Every falsifier in
     weighting.py, velocity.py and tier_rules.py says "visible the day
     impressions exist" — so none of those numbers can currently be
     proved wrong. This is V2_15 Phase 4.

  3  TWO RAILS HAVE ARROWS THAT GO NOWHERE. not-ordered-yet has
     service, endpoint and paging and no screen, so sections.py
     withholds the chevron rather than shipping one that leads nowhere.
     your-suppliers past the cap belongs in My Connections, which is a
     TAB with local state and no URL.

  4  EXPLORE WHOLESALERS HAS NO FRONT END. Service, routes, chips and
     the business-named-"Wholesalers" collision guard are all built and
     committed. Zero screens.

  5  NOTHING IN THE PRODUCT CAN SET A SUPPLIER'S PLAN. plan_tier exists
     with server_default FREE and nothing in src/ writes it — no CLI
     command, no admin route. The only MAX rows were set by a seed
     script. The tier ladder is real in code and unreachable in the
     product.

  6  SPONSORED HAS NO IMPRESSION OR CLICK RECORD. The slot is picked
     alphabetically, which is an honest placeholder and not a billable
     one.

  7  FULFILMENT HAS NO UI. mark_dispatched and confirm_received are
     built and tested; no screen calls them. V2_14's five questions are
     still open.


===========================================================================
PART 6 — AND ONE THAT LOOKED LIKE A SPONSORED BUG AND IS NOT
===========================================================================

The empty coloured block in the sponsored slot is not a sponsored
defect.

SupplierCard renders a 120px cover band. With no cover image AND no
logo it falls back to a flat surfaceVariant rectangle with a 56px
avatar centred in it. Every supplier currently seeded has neither
image. At the rail's 240px that block reads as a card; the sponsored
section stretches the same component to roughly 360px, and it becomes a
large empty rectangle.

The defect is on EVERY image-less supplier card. Sponsored only makes
it biggest.

THE SLOT STAYS. It is the thing MAX and PARTNER actually buy, and
deleting it deletes the top of the ladder V2_16 designed. What changes
is the rendering: the sponsored supplier gets its own HORIZONTAL
layout — logo left, name and tick and meta right — rather than a
stretched rail card, and the cover band is omitted entirely when there
is no image rather than being filled with colour.

PART 7 — BUILD ORDER
===========================================================================

  1  Sponsored card and the image-less supplier card.
     Visible immediately, no migration, no backend.

  2  Verification tab out. Trading Details time inputs to a picker.

  3  Business name persisted at registration (Part 3).

  4  setup-state endpoint, the three setup screens, the gate.

  5  not-ordered-yet screen.

  6  Migration dropping the two completeness columns — LAST, once
     nothing reads them.

Each step is a commit. Six is deliberately last: a column is dropped
only after the code that read it is already gone and proven gone,
because the reverse order is a broken deploy window.
