===========================================================================
V2_05 — HOME, AND THE DISCOVERY INGESTION ENGINE
===========================================================================

Depends on V2_03. Two tabs that look unrelated and are not: Home answers
"what needs me today", Discovery answers "what should I know". Both are
ranked, per-user, per-role feeds, and both should be built on the same
ranking seam rather than two.


===========================================================================
PART 1 — HOME
===========================================================================

1.1 THE STANDING INSTRUCTION, AND WHY IT WAS RIGHT

  business/(tabs)/home.tsx currently says:

      "Home — intentionally empty for now. Do NOT force features in here
       just because there is available space; Home becomes the daily
       business overview once enough business activity exists to make it
       useful, not before."

  That was correct and it is now satisfied. There IS activity: orders,
  connections, a loan book, an order book, a catalogue. Home can be
  filled without inventing anything.

1.2 THE SPECIFICATION WAS WRITTEN BY SOMEBODY ELSE

  From V2_03 Part 3, the line about what brings a shopkeeper back:

      their own prices · what they qualify for · what they owe ·
      where the last order is

  That is Home. Not a dashboard of charts — four questions, answered.

1.3 BUSINESS HOME

  Cards appear ONLY when they have something to say. A zero card is
  worse than no card — the rule the supplier Home already follows
  ("the card only renders when there's something real to show").

  1  NEEDS YOU TODAY
     Orders awaiting payment or confirmation, connection requests,
     anything blocking a delivery. One line each, tap straight through.
     This card is the reason to open the app on a day you are not
     buying.

  2  YOUR BOOK SAYS                          [REVISED — NOT "your money"]
     ONE number, from Loan Book: what this business's own book says it
     is owed by its customers.

     THE LABEL IS THE WHOLE POINT. We do not track this business's
     money — no till, no bank feed, no card machine. Loan Book is
     SELF-REPORTED: they may record every credit sale, or a third of
     them, and they may well use the tool for something we never
     imagined. Calling a partial, hand-kept list "your money" dresses
     it as a financial position it is not, and the first time it
     disagrees with the cash in the drawer the merchant stops trusting
     everything else on this screen.

     "Your book says R1,240 owed" is true. "You have R1,240" is not.

  3  WHERE MY ORDER IS
     Open stock orders with their real status. "Where is the last
     order" from the research, verbatim. These are PLATFORM-OBSERVED,
     so unlike card 2 we can state them flatly.

  4  TIME TO REORDER
     From the reorder-gap computation in V2_04. "You usually order from
     Sipho about now." One tap to a pre-filled basket of last order's
     lines — the single highest-leverage button in the app.

  5  PRICE CHANGED ON WHAT YOU BUY
     The catalogue rail, condensed to a line. Links into Community.

  A NEW USER SEES ALMOST NOTHING, and that is correct. Home earns its
  content. What a brand-new business sees instead is one card: finish
  your profile, find your first supplier.

1.4 SUPPLIER HOME

  Same shape, their questions.

  1  NEEDS YOU TODAY
     Orders to accept or decline, connection requests, deliveries due.
     Already partly built (customer requests card).
     SEE FULFILMENT MODE BELOW — a wholesaler on auto-accept sees none
     of the accept/decline items, and that is the point.

  2  WHAT I SOLD                             [REVISED — NOT "your money"]
     We hold no funds and settle nothing, so this is ORDER ACTIVITY,
     not a balance:

       orders received
       orders awaiting delivery
       deliveries due today
       value of orders placed — with DIGITALLY PAID shown separately
       from cash, because per V2_03 Part 3.3 only the first are
       confirmed. Cash is recorded; digital is proven.

  3  CUSTOMERS GOING QUIET
     From the reorder-due computation. Their own customers.

  4  SHOPFRONT HEALTH
     Offers with no price, unmatched import lines, shopfront closed,
     minimum order unset. The specific things stopping them selling.

1.5 FULFILMENT MODE — THE SETTING THAT REPLACES A DELETION

  A small supplier accepts and declines each order by hand. A wholesaler
  with an ERP does not, and asking them to is how the platform proves it
  does not understand their business.

  This is a SUPPLIER SETTING, not a feature removal:

      MANUAL      every order and connection needs a decision.
                  Today's behaviour. Default.

      AUTO        orders are accepted on placement and pushed to the
                  integration; connections auto-approve.
                  Requires: an API key with orders:write, a set minimum
                  order, and priced offers.

  It lands on SupplierProfile as a column and changes what Home shows,
  what notifications fire, and whether requires_connection is even
  offered. Everything downstream already exists — the API keys and
  orders:write scope shipped in step 7.

  ONE HONEST WARNING: auto-accept without stock awareness produces
  accepted orders a supplier cannot fill, which is worse than a slow
  decline. AUTO should require inventory tracking to be on, or an
  explicit "I always have stock" acknowledgement.

1.6 WHEN THE EXPECTED DELIVERY DATE IS ASKED FOR        [FROM REVIEW]

  The payment method decides the moment, and the difference is not
  cosmetic.

    CASH        The supplier taps Approve and is IMMEDIATELY prompted
                for the expected delivery date. There is no payment
                event to wait for, so approval is the commitment.

    DIGITAL     Approval and payment are separate moments. The date is
                captured AFTER payment is confirmed — because promising
                a date against money that has not arrived is how a
                supplier ends up apologising for something that was
                never their fault.

  In both cases the date, once given, fires an automatic in-app message
  to the business, and a WhatsApp utility template (V2_08 Part 3) at
  roughly R0.15 a message — which is the channel this market actually
  reads.

  WHY THIS BELONGS IN A HOME DOCUMENT: "deliveries due today" on the
  supplier's Home and "where is my order" on the business's Home are
  both empty unless this date exists. The prompt is what makes two
  cards on two different screens true.


===========================================================================
PART 2 — DISCOVERY: WHAT IT IS
===========================================================================

Community is relationships. Discovery is knowledge. The existing shell
already says so and already has the right tabs: ALL, LEARN, UPDATES,
OPPORTUNITIES.

The brief: news, YouTube video from creators, advice, opportunities,
TruConnect announcements, and later marketing or advertising. Different
per user and per role. An INGESTION ENGINE.

2.1 WHY THIS IS WORTH BUILDING NOW RATHER THAN LATER

  From V2_03 Part 4: hundreds of spaza shops changed hands through
  2025–2026, and the new owners have never run a shop. Bread went from
  ~R16 to ~R30 in some communities because people did not know how to
  price. Simultaneously, every one of them is legally required to
  register with their municipality, and the Spaza Shop Fund requires
  CIPC, SARS and UIF registration on top.

  There is a cohort of people right now who need to be taught the trade
  and told what paperwork they owe. That is Discovery's entire reason to
  exist, and the window is a year or two wide.

2.2 CONTENT SOURCES, IN ORDER OF HOW REAL THEY ARE TODAY

  TRUCONNECT ANNOUNCEMENTS      us. Always available. No dependency.
  OPPORTUNITIES                 Spaza Shop Fund, SEDA, SEFA, municipal
                                registration drives, supplier promos.
                                Human-curated. Small volume, high value.
  LEARN                         YouTube, from channels we choose.
  SUPPLIER PROMOS               a supplier's own announcement to
                                connected businesses. Cheap; uses the
                                notification infrastructure that exists.
  NEWS                          deliberately last. Feeds are easy to
                                ingest and hard to keep relevant, and a
                                stale news tab is a dead tab.


===========================================================================
PART 3 — THE YOUTUBE CONSTRAINTS, WHICH DECIDE THE ARCHITECTURE
===========================================================================

Researched rather than assumed, because both of these would have been
discovered late and painfully.

  QUOTA. The YouTube Data API v3 gives 10,000 units per project per day.
  search.list costs 100 units per call. That is ONE HUNDRED SEARCHES A
  DAY, total, for the whole platform.

  → It is therefore IMPOSSIBLE to call YouTube per user, or per feed
    load, or per refresh. At a thousand users the quota is gone before
    breakfast. Any design where the app or the request path talks to
    YouTube is dead on arrival.

  PLAYER POLICY. The embeddable player may not be modified, wrapped so
  as to block its functionality, or stripped of its links back to
  YouTube. Access to a video may not be gated behind any action other
  than pressing play, and users may not be charged to watch.

  → We may not screenshot thumbnails into our own carousel and play
    inline in a custom surface, and we may not put a video behind a
    "complete your profile" wall. We embed the official player.

3.1 WHAT THOSE TWO FACTS FORCE

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Ingestion is a SCHEDULED SERVER JOB writing into OUR OWN table.
  Ranking happens over OUR rows. The app never sees YouTube's API.
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  A nightly job pulls from a curated channel list (playlistItems.list
  costs 1 unit per call, not 100 — pulling a channel's uploads playlist
  is roughly a hundred times cheaper than searching, and channel-based
  ingestion is what we want anyway). Rows are stored with video id,
  title, channel, duration, published date, thumbnail URL and our own
  topic tags. The feed ranks those rows per user. Playback uses the
  official embed.

  This also gives editorial control, which searching would not: we
  choose the channels, so a business owner is never shown something
  irrelevant or unsuitable because a search matched.

3.2 THE CURATION PROBLEM, STATED HONESTLY

  Ingestion is the easy half. The hard half is that most YouTube content
  about "retail business" is American, generic, and useless to someone
  running a shop in Tembisa. A LEARN tab full of Harvard Business Review
  clips is a dead tab.

  Proposal: start with a HAND-PICKED CHANNEL LIST, small — ten to twenty
  South African small-business, township-economy and FMCG channels —
  and a human approval step before anything appears. Curation is a
  feature here, not overhead. Automate the pulling, never the choosing.


===========================================================================
PART 4 — THE INGESTION ENGINE
===========================================================================

4.1 SHAPE

  One domain: src/domains/platform/discovery/

    models/           content_item, content_source, content_impression
    services/         ingestion_service, ranking_service, curation_service
    providers/        youtube_provider, rss_provider (later)
    schedulers/       discovery_scheduler.py  → JOB_SPECS
    api/              discovery_routes.py

  PROVIDERS BEHIND AN ABSTRACTION, exactly as media, payment, geocoding
  and AI already are. ContentProvider with fetch() → list[ContentItem].
  YouTube is one implementation; RSS and a manual editor are others.
  Nothing outside providers/ imports the YouTube client. Add the
  boundary test with the domain, as with the feed service.

  THE SCHEDULER IS ALREADY BUILT. src/master_scheduler owns the single
  APScheduler instance and defines no jobs; a domain adds a JobSpec to
  its own schedulers/ file and registers the list in registry.py. This
  is exactly what that design was for.

4.2 THE CONTENT ITEM

  One table, all sources.

    id, source_id, external_id, kind (VIDEO | ARTICLE | ANNOUNCEMENT |
    OPPORTUNITY | PROMO), title, summary, thumbnail_url, url,
    published_at, duration_seconds, language, topics[], audience_roles[],
    regions[], status (PENDING | APPROVED | REJECTED | EXPIRED),
    approved_by, approved_at, expires_at

  NOTE audience_roles AND expires_at. An opportunity with a closing date
  must disappear on its own — a funding round that closed in March,
  still on screen in July, tells a user this app is abandoned. And
  status defaults to PENDING, which is the human approval step from 3.2
  made structural rather than procedural.

4.3 RANKING

  Deliberately simple, and deliberately transparent.

    role match          supplier content to suppliers, business to
                        businesses. Hard filter, not a weight.
    region match        province, then national.
    recency             decay over ~30 days.
    topic affinity      from what they have opened before.
    pinned              announcements we need everyone to see.

  NO ENGAGEMENT-MAXIMISING RANKING, EVER. The existing Discovery
  docstring already says the right thing: "curated, not an infinite
  feed — the business owner should find something useful and leave." A
  shopkeeper's attention is their working day. Optimising for time in
  app would be extracting value from the exact people this platform
  exists to help, and it would be the first genuinely dishonest thing in
  this product.

4.4 COST

  Zero, at this scale. YouTube Data API has no per-call charge, only the
  quota — and channel-based ingestion of twenty channels once a day is
  a few hundred units against ten thousand. No AI is required for
  ranking. If topic tagging ever wants a model, it goes through
  shared/ai with the spend guard that already exists.


===========================================================================
PART 5 — WHAT DISCOVERY MUST NOT BECOME
===========================================================================

  NOT A SOCIAL FEED. No posting, no comments, no likes. Moderation of
  user content in this market is a full-time function we do not have,
  and the first scam posted to a spaza owners' feed would cost more
  trust than the feature could ever earn.

  NOT INFINITE. A finite, dated, expiring list. If there are four
  useful things this week, show four.

  NOT WHERE ADVERTISING HIDES. When advertising arrives it is labelled,
  in the same way SPONSORED_SUPPLIER is already labelled in the
  Community contract, with a server-supplied label so disclosure wording
  can follow whatever the rules require.


===========================================================================
PART 6 — OPEN QUESTIONS FOR REVIEW
===========================================================================

  1  WHO APPROVES CONTENT? The status column implies a reviewer, and
     there is no admin control centre yet. MVP answer: a management
     command plus a seeded list, with the web control centre taking it
     over later. It must not be "whoever has database access".

  2  LANGUAGE. isiZulu, isiXhosa, Sesotho, Afrikaans, English. The
     settings screens already contemplate language. Does Discovery
     filter by the user's language from day one, or ship English-first
     and add filtering later? Recommendation: store the field now, filter
     later — retrofitting the column is the expensive half.

  3  DATA COST. Video thumbnails on a metered connection are a real cost
     to the user, and settings/mobile-data.tsx and data-usage.tsx already
     exist. Recommendation: Discovery respects the existing data-saver
     setting and does not autoplay anything, ever.

  4  IS "NEWS" WORTH IT AT ALL? Recommendation: no, not for MVP. Four
     curated videos and two real opportunities beat a news river nobody
     maintains.
