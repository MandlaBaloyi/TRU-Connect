1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

Millions of informal and small businesses across South Africa — spaza shops,
salons, restaurants, mechanics, street vendors, and small wholesalers — operate
entirely outside of the formal financial system.

They face a recurring set of problems:

1.1 Sourcing Problems
    - Limited visibility into which suppliers are reliable, competitively
      priced, or capable of delivering to their area.
    - No structured way to compare suppliers beyond word of mouth.
    - Time lost travelling to physical markets or wholesalers to place orders.

1.2 Trust Problems
    - No shared record of a supplier's reliability, delivery performance, or
      responsiveness.
    - No shared record of a business's own reliability as a customer.
    - Star ratings and reviews are easily manipulated and rarely reflect real
      commercial behaviour.

1.3 Data & Financial Invisibility
    - Informal businesses transact largely in cash and outside of any
      recorded system.
    - Because there is no digital trail of their commercial activity, they
      cannot demonstrate creditworthiness to banks or lenders.
    - Suppliers face the same problem: they cannot easily demonstrate
      reliability or scale to potential financial or institutional partners.

1.4 Supplier-Side Problems
    - Suppliers (FMCG suppliers, regional wholesalers, beverage suppliers,
      manufacturers, distributors, equipment and service providers) rely on
      manual order-taking, phone calls, and informal relationships.
    - Suppliers have no digital channel to reach new business customers or to
      demonstrate their own track record.

The core problem is not the absence of a marketplace. It is the absence of
trusted, verifiable digital infrastructure connecting businesses and
suppliers.

================================================================================
2. VISION
================================================================================

TRU Connect will become the trusted digital infrastructure connecting
businesses, suppliers, and — in time — financial institutions across South
Africa.

TRU Connect helps businesses discover trusted suppliers, build trusted
business relationships, and quietly creates the business data needed to
unlock future financial opportunities.

Every ordinary business activity — searching for a supplier, asking a
question, placing an order, receiving a delivery — becomes a data point in a
growing, trustworthy record of commercial behaviour.

================================================================================
3. MISSION
================================================================================

To give every informal and small business in South Africa:

    - A simple way to find and order from suppliers they can trust.
    - A digital business identity that grows automatically through everyday
      use, without requiring manual data entry or paperwork.
    - A pathway toward financial inclusion built on real commercial evidence
      rather than paperwork or collateral.

To give every supplier:

    - A digital storefront and order channel that reaches business customers
      directly.
    - A reputation system based on real transaction history rather than
      manipulable reviews.
    - Access, over time, to trade finance and working capital based on their
      demonstrated performance on the platform.

5. CORE PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

TRU Connect is built on one governing idea:

    Help businesses buy better, help suppliers sell more, and quietly build
    trusted business data in the background.

This philosophy has three direct implications for product design:

5.1 Invisible Data Collection
    Users should never feel like they are filling out a credit application
    or building a financial record. Every normal action — searching,
    asking a question, ordering — should feel like an ordinary part of
    running a business, while quietly contributing to a structured business
    profile behind the scenes.

5.2 Relationships Over Transactions
    Unlike a conventional marketplace, TRU Connect optimises for durable,
    repeat relationships between specific businesses and suppliers, not
    one-off transactions. Repeat behaviour is the strongest trust signal
    available and is treated as such throughout the product.

5.3 Trust Before Lending
    Financial products (credit, trade finance, working capital) are never
    the entry point. They are the eventual output of a long period of
    verified, trusted commercial activity. Trust must be earned and
    observed before it can be underwritten.

================================================================================
6. WHY INFORMAL BUSINESSES
================================================================================

Informal businesses are chosen as the primary user group because:

    - They represent an enormous, underserved segment of the economy with
      real, recurring purchasing needs (stock, supplies, equipment).
    - They are almost entirely excluded from formal credit and financial
      services today, despite being creditworthy in practice.
    - Their daily operational needs (finding suppliers, placing orders) are
      immediate and valuable enough to drive habitual use, independent of
      any future financial product.
    - Habitual use is exactly what is required to generate the volume and
      quality of behavioural data needed to build trusted business profiles.

================================================================================
7. WHY SUPPLIERS
================================================================================

Suppliers are included as a first-class user group, not an afterthought,
because:

    - A marketplace without engaged, responsive suppliers has nothing for
      businesses to discover.
    - Suppliers benefit directly and immediately from a digital order
      channel and reduced manual overhead, giving them a standalone reason
      to participate.
    - Supplier reliability (delivery performance, responsiveness, pricing
      consistency) is itself a critical trust signal that businesses need,
      and that only suppliers can generate through their own activity.
    - Over time, suppliers themselves become candidates for trade finance
      and working capital products, mirroring the trust-building journey of
      businesses.

================================================================================
8. WHY TRUST BEFORE LENDING
================================================================================

TRU Connect deliberately sequences its roadmap so that trust infrastructure
is built before any financial product is introduced.

    - Lending without a trust foundation is high-risk and unsustainable at
      the scale TRU Connect intends to operate.
    - Businesses and suppliers are more willing to engage with a tool that
      solves an immediate operational problem (sourcing, selling) than one
      that immediately positions itself as a financial or credit product.
    - A trust and data layer built from real, verified commercial activity
      produces far stronger risk signals than self-reported financial
      information.
    - This sequencing (utility first, trust second, credit third) is what
      allows TRU Connect to eventually underwrite financial products with
      confidence, rather than relying on traditional collateral or
      documentation-heavy processes that exclude the very businesses it
      aims to serve.

================================================================================
9. LONG-TERM VISION
================================================================================

TRU Connect is designed to evolve in four broad phases:

    Phase 1 — Establish the core marketplace: supplier discovery, ordering,
               verification, and business questions.
    Phase 2 — Introduce payments, wallet functionality, and business
               insights derived from platform activity.
    Phase 3 — Introduce credit, trade finance, and working capital products,
               underwritten using data generated in Phases 1 and 2.
    Phase 4 — Open the platform via partner APIs to financial institutions
               and other ecosystem partners, positioning TRU Connect as
               shared trust infrastructure for the informal economy.

Over time, TRU Connect aims to support millions of businesses and suppliers,
becoming the default trusted layer between informal commerce and the formal
financial system in South Africa.

================================================================================
10. SUCCESS METRICS
================================================================================

Success for TRU Connect is measured differently at each phase, but always in
terms of relationships and trust, not just transaction volume.

10.1 Phase 1 Metrics (Engagement & Trust Foundations)
    - Number of verified businesses and suppliers onboarded.
    - Number of active supplier-business relationships (repeat interactions
      between the same pair of accounts).
    - Order completion rate and repeat order rate.
    - Business Question engagement (questions asked, response rate, response
      time).
    - Supplier response rate and average response time.
    - Month-over-month retention of both businesses and suppliers.

10.2 Longer-Term Metrics (Trust & Financial Readiness)
    - Growth in Business Health Score / Digital Business Profile completeness
      across the user base.
    - Percentage of businesses with sufficient verified activity to be
      considered for future financial products.
    - Supplier reliability scores and their correlation with business
      retention.
    - Volume and value of orders flowing through the platform.
    - Eventual: credit product uptake, repayment performance, and trade
      finance volume, once Phase 3 is reached.

Ultimately, TRU Connect's success is measured by the depth and reliability
of the trusted commercial data it generates — the asset that makes every
later phase of the platform possible.
