================================================================================
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
================================================================================

Millions of informal and small businesses across South Africa -- spaza
shops, salons, restaurants, mechanics, street vendors, and small
wholesalers -- already trade successfully every day. They already know
their suppliers. Their suppliers already know them.

The problem is not that these relationships are missing.

The problem is that these relationships are managed with tools that were
never designed for business operations.

1.1 Operational Friction (the problem TRUConnect solves today)
    - Orders are placed by WhatsApp message, phone call, or SMS, then
      forgotten, misremembered, or lost in a chat thread.
    - Order details are re-explained on every call: quantities, prices,
      delivery address, payment reference.
    - Delivery confirmations are verbal. Disputes come down to memory.
    - Credit sales to customers are recorded in a paper book -- easily
      lost, damaged, miscounted, or disputed.
    - Stock reordering depends on the owner remembering what they bought
      last time and from whom.
    - Nothing is searchable. Nothing is summarised. Nothing carries over
      when a phone is lost or replaced.

1.2 The Consequence: Commercial Invisibility
    Because none of this activity is recorded in any structured form:
    - The business cannot demonstrate what it actually trades, to anyone.
    - The supplier cannot demonstrate its reliability or scale.
    - No external party can distinguish a disciplined, consistently
      trading business from an inconsistent one.

    This is not a credit problem. It is a trust infrastructure problem
    (Constitution TP-005, TP-077).

1.3 Supplier-Side Friction
    Suppliers (FMCG suppliers, regional wholesalers, beverage suppliers,
    manufacturers, distributors) experience the mirror image:
    - Orders arrive across WhatsApp, phone, and SMS with no single queue.
    - Order details must be transcribed manually, introducing errors.
    - Delivery and payment status live in a spreadsheet, a notebook, or
      an employee's memory.
    - There is no reliable record of which customers order consistently.

================================================================================
2. VISION
================================================================================

TRUConnect will become the digital trade infrastructure that informal
businesses and their existing suppliers use to run their daily trading
relationships.

    TRUConnect does not create relationships.
    TRUConnect digitizes them.

It is the first operational layer of the TruePoint ecosystem (TP-008).
Every completed order, confirmed delivery, and recorded payment makes the
underlying trade relationship visible, measurable, and reliable -- which
is what makes every later layer of the ecosystem possible.

================================================================================
3. MISSION
================================================================================

To give every informal and small business in South Africa:

    - A simple, fast, mobile way to run the supplier relationships they
      already have -- replacing scattered WhatsApp threads and paper
      records with one operational tool.
    - A record of who owes them money, so credit sales stop depending on
      a paper book.
    - A trading history that accumulates automatically through ordinary
      use, requiring no paperwork and no data entry beyond normal work.

To give every supplier:

    - One queue containing every incoming order, instead of orders
      scattered across three communication channels.
    - Reliable order, delivery, and payment records without manual
      transcription.
    - Operational tooling that scales with them as they grow
      (Free -> Pro -> Enterprise).

================================================================================
4. TARGET USERS
================================================================================

4.1 Primary Users -- Informal Businesses (Business Free)
    Spaza shops, informal traders, SMEs, local retailers, salons and hair
    studios, restaurants, mechanics, street vendors, small wholesalers.

    Business Free is the primary user of the entire platform
    (Constitution TP-086: Businesses First). Business functionality is
    free because the platform cannot build trusted infrastructure without
    active businesses (TP-046).

4.2 Secondary Users -- Suppliers (tiered)

    Supplier Free
        FMCG suppliers, regional wholesalers, beverage suppliers,
        distributors, and service providers who need to receive and
        fulfil orders reliably. Free because supplier participation is
        what makes the business experience complete.

    Supplier Pro
        Suppliers who have outgrown order-taking alone and need product
        management, staff accounts, reporting, and a web dashboard.

    Supplier Enterprise
        Suppliers operating at scale who need teams, advanced reporting,
        ERP integration, and API access.

4.3 Future Users
    Lenders and financial institutions -- only after trusted operational
    infrastructure exists (TP-084: Infrastructure Before Finance). They
    are out of scope for TRUConnect itself and belong to later TruePoint
    platform stages.

================================================================================
5. CORE PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

5.1 Digitize, Don't Introduce
    The strongest network in the informal economy already exists: the
    relationship between a business and the suppliers it already trades
    with (TP-056). TRUConnect strengthens that network rather than
    attempting to replace it with a marketplace.

5.2 Operational Value First
    Businesses adopt TRUConnect because it solves today's operational
    problems -- not because they want loans (TP-009). Every feature must
    answer: "What business problem does this solve today?"

5.3 Invisible Data Accumulation
    Users should never be asked to manually create information that can
    emerge naturally from ordinary use (TP-022). Placing an order,
    confirming a delivery, recording a payment -- each is a normal
    business action that happens to also produce trusted data.

5.4 Independent Value At Every Layer
    Businesses must gain value before suppliers exist on the platform.
    Suppliers must gain value before enterprise capabilities exist.
    Enterprise suppliers must gain value before lender services exist
    (TP-019). No future capability may become a prerequisite for today's
    value.

================================================================================
6. WHY INFORMAL BUSINESSES
================================================================================

    - They represent an enormous, underserved segment of the economy with
      real, recurring operational needs.
    - Their daily friction (orders, deliveries, credit sales) is severe
      enough that a tool which genuinely reduces it will be used every
      day -- and habitual use is precisely what produces meaningful
      trading history.
    - They are almost entirely invisible to the formal financial system
      today, despite trading reliably in practice.
    - They are the starting point of the entire ecosystem (TP-086).

================================================================================
7. WHY SUPPLIERS
================================================================================

    - A business's supplier relationships cannot be fully digitized if
      only one side is present. Order confirmation, delivery status, and
      payment acknowledgement all require the supplier.
    - Suppliers gain immediate, standalone value: one order queue instead
      of three channels, and far less manual transcription.
    - Supplier reliability (acceptance, fulfilment, delivery performance)
      is itself operational data that only suppliers can produce.
    - Supplier tiers create the platform's primary sustainable revenue
      path (TP-047) without restricting access to essential functionality.

================================================================================
8. WHY TRUST BEFORE LENDING
================================================================================

The Constitution fixes this sequence permanently (TP-023, TP-083, TP-084):

    Business Operations -> Trusted Relationships -> Digital Trade ->
    Behavioural Data -> Trust -> Intelligence -> Finance

    - Lending without a trust foundation is unsustainable at scale.
    - Businesses engage far more readily with a tool that solves an
      immediate operational problem than with one presenting itself as a
      financial product.
    - Trust built from real, observed commercial activity produces far
      stronger signals than self-reported financial information.

TRUConnect therefore contains NO lending, NO money custody, and NO credit
products. It produces the trusted operational record that later TruePoint
products (TRUScore, TRUWallet, TRUPayLater) eventually depend on.

================================================================================
9. WHAT TRUCONNECT IS NOT
================================================================================

    - NOT a marketplace. It does not exist to help businesses find
      suppliers they don't already know (TP-057).
    - NOT a payment processor or money holder. Payments flow directly
      from customer to supplier. TRUConnect records that a payment
      happened; it never holds funds.
    - NOT a lender. Financial products belong to later TruePoint stages.
    - NOT competing with Amazon, Takealot, or Alibaba. It competes with
      WhatsApp, phone calls, SMS, paper order books, handwritten
      invoices, memory, and Excel (TP-054).

================================================================================
10. SUCCESS METRICS
================================================================================

Success is measured in operational adoption first, and trust second --
never in transaction volume alone.

10.1 Phase 1 -- Operational Adoption
    - Number of businesses actively using TRUConnect weekly.
    - Number of existing supplier relationships digitized per business.
    - Orders placed through the platform vs. estimated orders still
      placed by WhatsApp/phone (friction displaced).
    - Loan Book entries recorded and settled (paper-book displacement).
    - Weekly retention of Business Free users.
    - Offline actions successfully synchronised (reliability under real
      network conditions).

10.2 Phase 2 -- Supplier Participation
    - Supplier Free accounts activated by invitation from a business.
    - Percentage of a business's suppliers present on the platform.
    - Supplier order acceptance and fulfilment rates.
    - Conversion from Supplier Free to Supplier Pro.

10.3 Longer Term -- Trust Formation
    - Depth and consistency of trading history per business.
    - Repeat-order rate within established relationships.
    - Payment record consistency.
    - Proportion of businesses with sufficient verified activity to be
      meaningful to a future TRUScore assessment.

Revenue metrics belong to Supplier Pro/Enterprise adoption, never to
monetising trust itself (TP-047).
