Purpose: Define the phased evolution of TRU Connect, ensuring each phase
builds directly on the trust and data foundations established by the
phases before it.

================================================================================
PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION: DISCOVERY, TRUST, AND ORDERING
================================================================================

Objective: Establish the core marketplace and the data foundations required
for every subsequent phase.

Scope:
    - Business and Supplier registration and authentication (email/
      password, Google).
    - Supplier Discovery (search, categories, nearby, featured, popular
      products, saved suppliers).
    - Product Catalogue management (supplier-facing).
    - Orders (placement, acceptance, rejection, completion, tracking,
      invoices).
    - Business Questions & Supplier Responses ("Business Conversations").
    - Verification (business and supplier).
    - Digital Business Profile and Supplier Profile (automatically
      generated, rule-based, per Document 07).
    - Business Updates (supplier announcements).
    - Notifications.

Exit Criteria (informing readiness for Phase 2):
    - Meaningful volume of verified businesses and suppliers actively
      transacting.
    - Sufficient repeat-order and relationship data flowing through the
      trust engine to support meaningful Business Health Score and
      Supplier Reputation calculations.
    - Stable, well-tested core workflows (Document 06) with low order
      dispute/rejection rates.

================================================================================
PHASE 2 — PAYMENTS, WALLET, AND INSIGHTS
================================================================================

Objective: Introduce financial transaction infrastructure and surface
richer insights back to businesses and suppliers, without yet introducing
credit or lending.

Scope:
    - Payments — in-app payment processing for orders, replacing or
      supplementing existing offline payment methods.
    - Wallet — a stored-value account for businesses and/or suppliers,
      enabling faster transactions and providing a foundation for future
      credit disbursement and repayment rails (Phase 3).
    - Business Insights — business-facing analytics building on the
      Digital Business Profile (e.g. spending trends, supplier
      diversification, seasonal patterns).
    - Supplier Analytics — supplier-facing analytics building on the
      Supplier Profile (e.g. demand trends, customer retention patterns,
      response-time benchmarking against category peers).

Dependencies on Phase 1:
    - Wallet and Payments rely on the Orders and Relationship data
      structures established in Phase 1 (Document 04).
    - Average Payment Time (Document 07) becomes materially more accurate
      once real payment timestamps are available via the Wallet/Payments
      module, rather than being proxied by order completion time.

Exit Criteria:
    - Reliable, audited payment and wallet transaction history.
    - Adoption of in-app payments by a meaningful share of active
      relationships, generating a genuine (not proxied) payment-behaviour
      signal for the trust engine.

================================================================================
PHASE 3 — CREDIT, TRADE FINANCE, AND WORKING CAPITAL
================================================================================

Objective: Introduce financial products, underwritten using the trust and
payment data accumulated in Phases 1 and 2.

Scope:
    - Credit — short-term credit facilities for businesses (e.g. stock
      financing) based on Business Health Score and order history.
    - Trade Finance — financing instruments supporting larger or
      recurring supplier transactions.
    - Working Capital — working capital facilities for suppliers based on
      their Supplier Reputation and completed-order history.
    - Financial Products — packaging of the above into user-facing
      offerings, potentially in partnership with external financial
      institutions (anticipating Phase 4).

Dependencies on Phases 1–2:
    - Underwriting models consume the Trust Signals, Risk Signals, and
      Relationship Health data defined in Document 07, plus real payment
      behaviour data from Phase 2.
    - "Credit Readiness" thresholds (Document 07, Section 13) determine
      initial eligibility, ensuring credit is only extended where
      sufficient verified commercial history exists — consistent with the
      "Trust Before Lending" principle (Document 00).

Exit Criteria:
    - Defined, tested underwriting/risk model with acceptable performance
      on historical platform data.
    - Regulatory and compliance readiness for offering credit products in
      relevant jurisdictions.
    - Initial credit product performance (repayment rates) validating the
      underlying trust engine's predictive value.

================================================================================
PHASE 4 — PARTNER APIS, FINANCIAL INSTITUTIONS, AND OPEN PLATFORM
================================================================================

Objective: Position TRU Connect as shared, open trust infrastructure
connecting the informal economy to the broader financial system.

Scope:
    - Partner APIs — controlled, permissioned API access allowing external
      partners to integrate with TRU Connect's business/supplier data
      (subject to consent and privacy rules, Document 09).
    - Financial Institutions — direct integrations enabling banks, lenders,
      and insurers to originate products (credit, insurance, savings)
      using TRU Connect's trust data, with TRU Connect / TruePoint acting
      as the verified data and relationship layer rather than the sole
      lender.
    - Open Platform Expansion — potential extension of the ecosystem to
      adjacent services (e.g. logistics partners, insurance providers)
      built on the same trust infrastructure.

Dependencies on Phases 1–3:
    - Requires a mature, well-validated trust engine (Document 07) and a
      demonstrated track record of credit performance from Phase 3.
    - Requires robust data governance, consent, and privacy
      infrastructure to support external data sharing responsibly.

Exit Criteria:
    - Signed institutional partnerships with clearly defined data-sharing
      and revenue arrangements.
    - Scalable, secure partner API infrastructure independently audited
      for security and data protection compliance.

================================================================================
CROSS-PHASE ARCHITECTURAL COMMITMENTS
================================================================================

To ensure each phase can be built without reworking earlier phases, the
following commitments apply from Phase 1 onward:

    1. Database schema (Document 04) explicitly reserves entities/fields
       for Wallet, Credit, and Risk Signal data, even though they are
       unused until Phases 2–3.
    2. API versioning (Document 05) uses a stable, versioned base path to
       allow additive expansion without breaking existing mobile clients.
    3. The trust engine (Document 07) is built on transparent, rule-based
       calculations first, to keep early risk indicators explainable
       before any machine-learning-based refinement is introduced.
    4. Business Privacy and Supplier Visibility rules (Document 09) are
       enforced from Phase 1 onward, anticipating the heightened data-
       governance requirements of Phases 3–4.
