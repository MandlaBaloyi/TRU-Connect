--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHANGES IN THIS VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The previous roadmap placed Payments, Wallet, Credit, and Trade Finance
INSIDE TRUConnect (Phases 2-3). That is now incorrect.

TRUConnect is not a money holder and not a lender. Wallet and credit
capabilities belong to separate future TruePoint products (TRUWallet,
TRUPayLater) at platform stages beyond TRUConnect's own scope.

This roadmap is restructured to match the Constitution's staged ecosystem
model (TP-052), and covers only TRUConnect.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GOVERNING RULE (TP-052, TP-074)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every stage strengthens the stage above it. No stage may bypass the
layer beneath it. Operational value always precedes financial value.

================================================================================
STAGE 1 -- BUSINESS FREE  [CURRENT]
================================================================================

Objective: an informal business gets real operational value from
TRUConnect on day one, WITHOUT requiring any supplier to join.

Scope:
    - Authentication, OTP verification, password reset            [DONE]
    - Notification infrastructure (email)                         [DONE]
    - Business Profile and Profile Setup
    - My Suppliers: add existing suppliers (on- or off-platform)
    - Orders recorded against any supplier, on-platform or not
    - Order Queue
    - Repeat Order
    - Loan Book: credit sales, repayments, outstanding balance
    - Customers (minimal, supporting the Loan Book)
    - Offline support with sync
    - Verification (business)

Exit criteria:
    - Businesses using the app weekly without prompting.
    - Meaningful proportion of a business's supplier relationships
      recorded.
    - Loan Book actively replacing paper for real users.
    - Offline sync demonstrably reliable on real networks.

Critical test of this stage: does a business still find TRUConnect
valuable if NONE of its suppliers ever join? If not, the stage has
failed, and Stage 2 must not begin.

================================================================================
STAGE 2 -- SUPPLIER FREE
================================================================================

Objective: businesses' existing suppliers join, so orders flow digitally
end-to-end rather than being one-sided records.

Scope:
    - Supplier registration and profile
    - Supplier invitation flow from businesses
    - Automatic relationship linking on supplier registration
    - Single Order Queue for suppliers
    - Accept / decline with reason
    - Order status progression through dispatch
    - Payment reference issuance
    - Delivery confirmation (both parties)
    - Payment events (marked sent / confirmed)
    - Supplier verification
    - Supplier performance profile

Dependencies: Stage 1 must have real business adoption. Suppliers join
because their customers are already there -- not the reverse.

Exit criteria:
    - Suppliers joining primarily via business invitations.
    - Growing share of orders bilaterally confirmed rather than
      one-sided.
    - Delivery and payment confirmation rates high enough to be
      meaningful trust signals.

================================================================================
STAGE 3 -- SUPPLIER PRO
================================================================================

Objective: first sustainable revenue, earned by solving genuinely harder
supplier problems (TP-047).

Scope:
    - Product catalogue management
    - Businesses order against real products and prices (removing
      free-text ordering errors and price disputes)
    - Staff users with scoped permissions
    - Supplier reporting (orders, customers, fulfilment)
    - Web dashboard (frontend/web)
    - Tier management and upgrade/downgrade flows

Dependencies: enough supplier order volume that catalogue management and
staff access become genuine pain points. Introducing paid tiers before
that pain exists would violate TP-045 (Trust Before Revenue).

================================================================================
STAGE 4 -- SUPPLIER ENTERPRISE
================================================================================

Objective: serve suppliers operating at scale.

Scope:
    - Teams and role hierarchies across branches/regions
    - Advanced reporting and export
    - ERP integration
    - Partner API access
    - Higher-volume operational tooling

Dependencies: Supplier Pro suppliers demonstrably outgrowing Pro.

================================================================================
STAGE 5 -- PLATFORM MATURITY (WITHIN TRUCONNECT)
================================================================================

Objective: deepen operational value and data quality; prepare
TRUConnect's outputs for consumption by later platform capabilities.

Scope:
    - Richer business insights (operational, not financial)
    - Trust signal maturity and methodology refinement
    - Event-driven architecture between domains (TP-036)
    - Real-time updates via WebSockets for order and delivery status
      (TP-037)
    - Supplier Discovery  [the small, late-phase feature]
        For the occasional genuine need for a NEW supplier: a new product
        line, a supplier that closed, relocation. Lives in Discovery.
        Deliberately secondary; never the primary way to add a supplier.
    - Performance and scale hardening

================================================================================
BEYOND TRUCONNECT -- FUTURE TRUEPOINT PLATFORM STAGES
================================================================================

The following are SEPARATE PRODUCTS at the TruePoint platform level. They
are listed here only so TRUConnect's architecture does not preclude them.
They are NOT TRUConnect features and are NOT built inside TRUConnect.

    Stage 6 -- TRUScore
        Platform-level trust scoring, consuming TRUConnect trust signals
        alongside signals from other products.

    Stage 7 -- TRUWallet
        Financial infrastructure. TRUConnect remains outside the payment
        path regardless.

    Stage 8 -- TRUPayLater
        Credit products, built upon trusted infrastructure and TRUScore.

Constitutional constraint (TP-084): no financial capability may precede
the operational infrastructure and trust it depends on. TRUConnect's job
is to build that foundation well, and nothing more.

================================================================================
CROSS-STAGE ARCHITECTURAL COMMITMENTS
================================================================================

    1. Single identity, RBAC-driven capability. New roles and tiers are
       added as data, never as new user tables (TP-032).
    2. Additive migrations only. Nothing implemented is dropped or
       renamed (TP-014, TP-040).
    3. Versioned APIs; backward compatibility preserved (TP-035, TP-043).
    4. Domain isolation maintained, enabling event-driven evolution
       without rewrite (TP-033, TP-034, TP-036).
    5. Offline capability treated as a first-class requirement at every
       stage, not retrofitted (TP-062).
    6. Trust methodology versioned from the first calculation, so
       historical scores remain interpretable.
