a cleaner architecture for TRU Connect. I would separate the rate-limit mechanism from the rate-limit policies owned by each domain.

Something like:

src/
└── domains/
    ├── security/
    │   └── rate_limit/
    │       ├── __init__.py
    │       ├── limiter.py
    │       └── responses.py
    │
    ├── auth/
    │   └── rate_limit/
    │       └── policies.py
    │
    ├── business/
    │   └── rate_limit/
    │       └── policies.py
    │
    ├── supplier/
    │   └── rate_limit/
    │       └── policies.py
    │
    ├── loan_book/
    │   └── rate_limit/
    │       └── policies.py
    │
    └── order_book/
        └── rate_limit/
            └── policies.py
The idea

security/rate_limit/ owns the engine:

security/rate_limit/
├── limiter.py       ← Flask-Limiter instance/config
└── responses.py     ← common 429 handling

The individual domains own their policies:

auth/rate_limit/policies.py
        ↓
LOGIN = "5 per minute"
REGISTER = "5 per hour"
OTP_REQUEST = "5 per 10 minutes"

Then:

business/rate_limit/policies.py
        ↓
BUSINESS_READ = "120 per minute"
BUSINESS_UPDATE = "20 per minute"

And eventually:

loan_book/rate_limit/policies.py
        ↓
CUSTOMERS_READ = "120 per minute"
PAYMENT_CREATE = "30 per minute"
Why I prefer this for TRU Connect

Because rate-limit numbers are domain-specific.
